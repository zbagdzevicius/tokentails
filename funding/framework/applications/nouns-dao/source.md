What makes a good proposal — your proposal should communicate:
- "What is the project"
- "How will it proliferate Nouns"
- "How much funding you'll need"
- "How the funds break down"
- "What are the success metrics"

Successful proposals include "a simple overview, a timeline with milestones, and a cost breakdown."

"If you don't own a Noun yourself then you'll have to get a sponsor to open the proposal for you."
Sponsors can be found through Discourse feedback, Discord, The Nouncil, or Nouns SubDAOs.

Recommended: a "TLDR section" at the beginning — "if they don't read the full proposal, what do
they need to know?"

Proposals undergo "budget & impact scrutiny", focused on whether "the proposal proliferates Nouns
effectively based off the proposed execution and funds requested."
