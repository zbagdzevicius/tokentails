Token Tails is a cat-rescue game from a Lithuanian studio [F-021]. Players adopt and care for characters based on real shelter cats, and every cat's story and art come from an AI pipeline: OpenAI classifies each cat and writes its story, and Gemini paints its avatar and 4K portrait [F-019].

With a Game3 grant we will turn the AI pipeline from a character generator into a content engine that generates new levels, events and story arcs around each rescued cat, so the game gains fresh content every week without a larger team.

## Problem Statement <!-- field: problem | criterion: C4 -->
Shelter cats wait for homes because few people ever see them. A game built around real animals can change that, but only if it keeps giving players new stories, levels and art to come back for, and a small studio cannot hand-make that content fast enough. Today our AI pipeline creates each cat's character [F-019], while levels and events are still made by hand.

## Your Solution <!-- field: solution | criterion: C1, C2, C6 -->
Token Tails turns each real shelter cat into a playable character: OpenAI classifies the cat and writes its story, and Gemini paints its avatar and 4K portrait [F-019]. The game already runs on the web, iOS and Android [F-015] [F-016] with five game modes [F-018]. The grant extends the pipeline from characters to content, in three milestones:

- M1, months 1–2: an AI level generator for the match-3 mode that builds new levels from each cat's story, with human review before release.
- M2, months 3–4: weekly AI-written story events around real shelter cats, shipped on web, iOS and Android.
- M3, months 5–6: open the character pipeline to partner shelters so they can add their own cats, with a public changelog of every content release.
