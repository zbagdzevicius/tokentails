us in front of SKALE BD and the future grant committee.

## Benchmark

- **Stated priorities.** The SKALE team names its BD focus as "Agentic Payments and Privacy"
  (thread post 3). Its most recent money went to x402 agent-commerce teams (SuperPage, Legasi):
  https://www.skale.space/blog/san-francisco-agentic-commerce-x402-hackathon-recap-winners
- **Past game grants.** These include the $5M SKALE MP (2022), the GDC grant ($2M, 2024), the
  Indie Game Accelerator ($2M, 2025, milestone payouts) and CryptoChronic (2024, amount not
  disclosed). Every one of them went to games that ship on SKALE. The published criteria were: "time
  to market, quality of work, innovation, size of following/brand awareness, and unique value they
  can bring to the SKALE ecosystem."
- **The bar.** The team cites SKALE's gaming history as "66M UAWs and over 2.3B transactions"
  (post 10). We have no live activity on SKALE today.

## Payout mechanics (as far as they exist)

- **What the vote creates.** SIP-6 creates a maximum allocation of 288,750,000 SKL. It covers
  grants, engineering, BD, marketing and liquidity.
- **Who decides.** "Grant decisions involve an active grant committee consisting of different
  ecosystem participants" (post 10). No members, criteria or schedule are published.
- **How past grants paid.** Earlier SKALE game grants paid in SKL, sometimes through milestone
  tranches (Indie Accelerator). They were applied for by Typeform or a program page. Expect SKL,
  milestone-based and tied to shipping on SKALE. There is no fiat, and no route exists today.
- **What we can get from this post.** Nothing directly. The only upside is visibility with the
  people who will run the round.
