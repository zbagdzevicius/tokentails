## The proposal (post 1, 2026-09-15, SKALE team)

Title: "SIP-6: The 4-Year Terminal Tokenomics & Protocol Growth Framework" (category Governance).
Replaces the long-tail emission schedule of the remaining unminted SKL with a 4-year sprint to zero
inflation (October 2026 to September 2030), in two parts: a declining validator runway and a
Strategic Growth Allocation to the Foundation for ecosystem grants, core engineering, BD, marketing
and liquidity. Verbatim bullet:

> "Ecosystem & Developer Grants: Attracting high-throughput applications and infrastructure tooling."

What the author asked for, verbatim:

> "We look forward to hearing the community's thoughts, feedback, and discussion on this
> restructuring before we move to a formal governance vote."

The vote was planned before the 2026-10-01 epoch start. Update 2026-09-28: KuCoin community news (2026-09-22) reports "SIP-6 is live on Snapshot" (https://www.kucoin.com/news/community/SKL/6ab28b0e74fd460007c4c60f); the Snapshot space skale.eth uses a 7-day period, so voting likely ends about 2026-09-29. ## Replies (13 posts, last 2026-09-26)

Reviewer re-read 2026-09-27: the topic now shows 16 posts, so replies have arrived since this summary. Read the new ones before posting and adjust the reply if the committee or criteria question has been answered.

- A community member asked for fee-revenue targets and annual milestones to judge whether the growth
  allocation moves the network toward a fee-sustained economy, and later suggested "fee revenue per
  node relative to the cost of running that node" as the metric.
- A SKALE team member answered that fixed dollar targets do not fit because revenue is tied to the
  node count; the goal is to rank back into the top 20 chains by fee revenue, then top 10. Verbatim:
  "That's why our BD and marketing efforts have shifted so heavily toward Agentic Payments and
  Privacy."
- The proposal author explained agents use SKALE on Base with Credits, no KYC ("Agents don't need to
  be KYC'd for buying Credits - same as 'real' people.") and that validator revenue is trackable on
  Dune.
- One supportive reply ("4-year sprint", fighting spirit).
- A critical reply (highest engagement among critics) demanded "strict KPIs, vesting schedules, and
  independent multisig controls" and milestone-based unlocks. A later post by the same member was
  flagged and hidden.
- The proposal author answered (post 10, 2026-09-17), verbatim: "The 288M SKL represents a maximum
  allocation"; "Grant decisions involve an active grant committee consisting of different ecosystem
  participants."; "Historically, SKALE has ranked among the leading gaming chains, with more than
  66M UAWs and over 2.3B transactions." Also: existing multisig, Liechtenstein foundation, annual
  third-party audit.
- Later replies (2026-09-25, 2026-09-26) asked for concrete current fee revenue and burn numbers and
  questioned competition messaging.

## What gets traction / what is missing

- Traction: accountability. KPIs, milestone-based release and public on-chain measurement are the
  recurring asks; the team answers with Dune-trackable revenue and gaming history.
- Missing in the thread: grant criteria, an application route, and any definition or measurement
  of "high-throughput applications". The team's stated BD focus is agentic payments and privacy,
  so consumer games are not named, even though the author cites SKALE's gaming history.
- Background (not from the thread): SKALE ran an ecosystem grant program with a committee in 2022
  (https://skale.space/blog/skale-100million-usd-ecosystem-incentive-program).
