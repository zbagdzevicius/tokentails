**What it is.** Token Tails is a cat-rescue game live on web, iOS and Android. We built ShelterSplit, an open MIT payout rail: one contract call splits a USDC payment across registered animal shelters and emits a public event per payout. Now any AI agent can pay it through x402.

**What x402 unlocks.** {X402_URL} sells a shelter cat card (a real cat's story and portrait) for a small USDC price, using the standard `exact` scheme through a facilitator. The payment is split on-chain between the shelter and the treasury. It is new demand for shelters: agents and apps that would never set up a bank donation can now pay a shelter with one request, a 402 and one paid retry. First mainnet payment: {PAY_TX}.

**Live on mainnet.** Endpoint {X402_URL}; contract and payouts listed at https://tokentails.com/shelter-payouts; source at https://github.com/zbagdzevicius/tokentails (MIT, Foundry tests).

**Disclosure.** The first shelter is Pink Paw (Rožinė pėdutė). Its receiving wallet is held by Token Tails on behalf of the shelter until handover; every payout into it is public.

**What the grant pays for.** Onboarding the next shelters with wallets they hold themselves, a facilitator-backed x402 example in our open SDK (ShelterSplit Rail), and a public dashboard of agent payments per shelter.
