# Team1 Avalanche mini grants — call rules

Track A fields: `chain` is a key in `tracks/a-build/chains.json` (or a list, any of which counts),
`mainnet_required: true` makes `fund check` demand a recorded mainnet deployment on that chain,
`repo` must be the public repo URL before `ready`. `profile` names `tracks/a-build/programs/<profile>.json`
(submission sections and limits); if that file does not exist the draft sections are used as-is.

## Scoring criteria

No published rubric. The criteria below are the only judging statements we have (RULES-COMPLIANCE §2.8).

| ID | Criterion | Weight | Verbatim quote |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Shipping on Avalanche | not stated | "fund builders who are shipping on Avalanche" |
| C2 | Long-term commitment to Avalanche | significant | "That commitment is what the committee puts significant weighting on." |

## Limits

From the apply form (https://build.avax.network/grants/team1-mini-grants/apply): project description up to 280
characters; Summary, Milestones and Why You Deserve up to 4,000 characters each; amount up to $10,000 in
whole dollars. The form has no image upload (put images in a hosted deck under "Other links").

## Mandatory annexes

- X profile (required by the form)
- Builder Hub sign-in and the consent "I consent to the sharing of my data with Team1."

## Exclusions

- A project that "belongs to another program or hackathon": create a new project, "ShelterSplit on Avalanche (Token Tails)".
- Accelerator Grants (up to $30,000) are nomination-only; this is the Mini Grant.

## Eligibility gates

- Shipping on Avalanche: the C-Chain split is live on mainnet. Never claim EURC on the C-Chain.
