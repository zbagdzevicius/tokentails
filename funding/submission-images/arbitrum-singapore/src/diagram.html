How ShelterSplit pays shelters
One transaction splits a USDC payment across registered shelters. The contract holds nothing between calls.

Who pays

  • Token Tails, from its own wallet: the test payouts shown here; sponsored game treats run on Arc testnet first
  • Any app or AI agent, through the MIT Rail SDK and donate widget
