How ShelterSplit works

One call. Every shelter paid.

An app purchase becomes a stablecoin payout that anyone can check. The buyer never needs a wallet.

1

A player buys

Card or in-app purchase in Token Tails (web, iOS, Android). In the planned flow, a fixed share goes to shelters.

2

Ops wallet pays

Token Tails' operations wallet calls disburse(amount, memo) in USDC or EURC. The memo carries the purchase reference.

3

ShelterSplit on Avalanche C-Chain

A public registry: each shelter has a wallet, a name and a share in basis points. The amount is split in the same transaction, so the contract holds nothing between calls.

Shelter wallets

Each active shelter gets its share, with one Disbursed event each.

Treasury

The rest, plus rounding dust.

Anyone checks

Every payout is an on-chain event. The receipt page on tokentails.com reads it from the chain, names the shelter and links to the block explorer.
