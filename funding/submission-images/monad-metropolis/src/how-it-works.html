How a treat reaches a shelter
1

Player

Wins a round of Catnip Heist and taps "Send Pink Paw a rescue treat".

No wallet. No gas.

2

Token Tails
backend

Checks the player (verified email, account older than a day, one treat a day), then pays from a capped daily USDC budget.

3

ShelterSplit
contract

disburse(amount, memo) splits the payment across registered shelter wallets by basis points. Rounding dust goes to a treasury. Holds nothing.

4

Pink Paw

Rožinė pėdutė, the first shelter. Its wallet is held by Token Tails on the shelter's behalf until handover.

One public event per payout

Amount, shelter wallet and a memo with no personal data, on the chain for anyone to check.

Receipt + share card

Each treat links to its transaction and a block explorer.

Payouts page

Lists every shelter and payout, read from chain events, not from our database.
