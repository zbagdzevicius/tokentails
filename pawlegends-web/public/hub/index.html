Deaths punish more, kills give much more XP (sim v35, balance v36) patch notes hosted

Every death makes the next respawn longer (+12% a death, up to +72%; one death forgiven per 1:30 alive), respawns grow faster through the match (caps 40 s in 3v3, 55 s in 5v5), and a champion kill gives about 1.8× the XP, so a 10-kill champion is 2.5 to 3 levels above one with none. The nap card shows "Death 4 · +36%". Practice dummies no longer block the Fish Mill. Sim v36 balance update: every champion now wins 46–53% in 3v3 and 48–52% in Classic 5v5.

Five 3v3 maps (sim v34) patch notes hosted

Skirmish and 3v3 PvP draw one of five maps each match: Purrhaven Crossing, Lanternfall Ridge (one lane, the Fish Mill), Twinbark Thicket (two Paw Shrines), Geode Quarry (Catnip Gems and the Geode Surge) and Pawball Park (kick the yarn ball into their goal). Practice picks any of them. Screenshots on desktop and phone, test numbers per map.

Kills and respawns (sim v33) patch notes hosted

Kills decide more: kill XP 120 + 30 a victim level, the killer +50%, bounties from a 2-streak and bigger shutdowns, so a 10-0 player is 2 to 3 levels ahead (was ~1.5). Respawns grow +8% a minute from 3:00, so a won late fight takes towers. Shutdown and bounty tags in the kill feed.
