## What was built

1. **Lobby (`components/game/GameSelect.tsx`, `components/impact/`)**
   - `RescueTile`: badge is lifetime paws (99+ cap) or NEW until IMPACT was opened once
     (`tt.rescue.seen`, try/catch storage). Tap calls `openProgress(setOpenedModal, "impact")`.
     Its heart pulse follows the strip's pause control (one lobby pause state in GameSelect).
   - `progressTab.ts`: `openProgress` keeps the existing `setOpenedModal(GameModal.CODEX)` path and
     carries the tab beside it: `takeProgressTab()` (once, 5 s TTL) for a Codex that mounts, and a
     `tt:progress-tab` window event `{ tab }` for one already mounted. **Task 6a**: Codex must call
     `takeProgressTab()` on mount and listen to `PROGRESS_TAB_EVENT`.
   - MEET SHELTER CATS: a button under RESCUE on phones, its own tile under HOME from md up, with a visible MEET SHELTER CATS caption under the art; both
     call `setGameType(GameType.SHELTER)` (the 2f crash-proof storefront path), one tap.
   - `ImpactStrip` (md+, hidden under 640 px height): today's paw plus treats and payouts via
     `<Claim>` with tier chips; static, `aria-live="off"`, a pause control for the only motion (the
     heart pulse; reduced motion never starts it). Empty state is future tense.
   - `PawProgress`: "N more run(s) for today's paw", run dots, countdown to the end of the UTC paw
     day in local time (null during SSR, and hidden when more than 24 h away so a skewed clock never
     shows "60h left today").
   - `useImpactMe` (`GET /impact/me`, never called for guests) and `useDonateRail`
     (`GET /shelter/donate/status`), one react-query entry each.
2. **ProfileModal**: MY IMPACT is `MyImpactSummary`, always shown under the STATS plates (no tab); the MY IMPACT button opens PROGRESS on IMPACT directly. It shows (paws, today's line, IN-GAME chip, OPEN
   IMPACT). The `spent > 0` gate and the spend-to-surgeries conversion are gone;
   `calculateDonationBreakdown` is deleted (no references remain). Renders with `spent=0` and
   `cat=null`.
3. **End of run** (`EndGameModal`, `PixelRescueEndGameModal`): LEVEL CLEARED when
   `lastOutcome.clearedNow` (read defensively until 5a lands `lastOutcome` on `useGame`);
   `EndGamePaw` re-reads `/impact/me` and says "Paw earned: tonight Token Tails pays a treat to
   Pink Paw." only when earned and `pawSettlements.sendEnabled`, otherwise progress; the treat CTA
   only when the rail is live with treats left and `instantTreat.eligible`.
4. **Tone sweep** with `shared-contracts/copy.ts` (`formatTails`, "Tails", no `$TAILS`): GameStats,
   Wheel (+ published odds from `GET /user/catbassadors/lives/odds`), GameOptions, MysteryBoxCat,

   payouts count from), or no date if unset (`payoutsEmptyCopy`, `claimsDateLabel`); per-contract

   the same tick as `setGameStop`, or pass `outcome` into `gameStop`, so a won replay after a clear
   cannot reuse the old object.
4. e2e no-scroll: it now also checks that no ancestor of the lobby scrolls, and that PLAY, MY PETS, HOME,
   RESCUE, the cat and MEET SHELTER CATS (button, or tile at md+) are inside the viewport.
