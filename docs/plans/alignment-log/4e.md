## Known limitations

- Until the backfill and `TAILS_EARNED_BACKFILL_DONE=true`, the earned board sorts on the balance.
  That is exact only while no pledge exists, which is why the pledge route must wait (step 3).
- The nap cap can refuse all of a burst of parallel stakes (never more than 3 kept); the player taps
  again. A finished but uncollected nap holds its slot.
- Tiers past Explorer need gives, so they are unreachable until Rescue Goals open (the G5 risk
  "without a goal budget the IMPACT tab shows its empty state").
- Past staking abuse still inflates `tails` and `tailsEarned` for flagged users (no clawback); they are
  only kept off the boards, and only after step 2 runs.
- The copy-lint subprocess test skips when `tools/copy-lint` has no `node_modules` (the CI backend job);
  the CI `copy-lint` job covers the same files there.
