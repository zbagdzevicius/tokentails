## What was built

- `client/scripts/sync-fonts.mjs` copies the woff2 files and each family's OFL licence to three places:
  - **client** (`client/public/fonts/`): Passion One 400/700/900, Bebas Neue 400 and Nunito variable (upright and italic), each in latin and latin-ext, plus Roboto 500 in latin only.
  - **CMS** (`cms/public/fonts/`): Passion One, Bebas Neue and upright Nunito.
  - **Heist** (`catnip-heist/public/fonts/`): upright Nunito only.

  It also writes:
  - `client/styles/fonts.generated.scss` and `cms/styles/fonts.generated.scss`. These hold our own `@font-face` rules: family names unchanged, `font-display: swap`, and Fontsource `unicode-range`. They also hold metric-matched `"<Family> Fallback"` faces: local Arial with `size-adjust` and ascent, descent and line-gap overrides from capsize.
  - `client/components/typography/fonts.generated.ts`, a pure-data manifest: `FONT_FILES`, `FALLBACK_FAMILIES`, `PRELOAD_FONT_FILES` and the source versions.
  - `catnip-heist/src/ui/fonts.generated.ts`, which exports `HEIST_FONT_FILES`, `HEIST_BODY_FONT` and `heistFontFaceCss(fontsBase)`.
- The Google `@import` is deleted from `client/styles/globals.scss`, which now `@use`s the generated SCSS. The html and portrait stacks name the fallback faces.
- `client/pages/_document.js` preloads Passion One 900, Bebas Neue 400 and Nunito (latin). After `load`, it warms those faces with `document.fonts.load`.
- The CMS `globals.scss` drops both Google `@import`s. The new `cms/pages/_document.tsx` sets `lang="en"` and preloads Passion One 400 and Bebas Neue 400.
- `client/components/typography/` has no Phaser import, and ESLint enforces this:
  - `roles.ts`: `TYPE_ROLES`, built from `design/tokens.ts` (1c), not from a second table. Also `roleSize`, `applyRoleCase` (sentence case for `hint`), `roleFaces` and `FONT_STACKS`.
  - `loadGameFonts.ts`: SSR-safe and memoised. It calls `document.fonts.load` once per face and never `check`. An empty match list counts as an undeclared face. It times out after 3000 ms, never rejects, and sends at most one `game_font_fallback` through the consent-gated `analytics` client.
  - `canvasFont.ts`: `ttCanvasFont(role, size)`.
- `client/components/Phaser/typography/`:
  - `TTFontsFile.ts`: `TTFontsFile extends Phaser.Loader.File`, plus `preloadTTFonts(scene)`. The loader counts the fonts file as a success even when fonts fall back. It writes the result to registry key `ttFontsResult`.
  - `ttText.ts`: `ttText`. `keepLinearFilter` re-applies LINEAR after every `updateText`, because Phaser re-uploads the texture with the game's NEAREST filter.
  - `ttStyle.ts`: `ttStyle` and `ttResolution`. Resolution comes from 2e's `textResolutionFor(camera.zoom, canvasPixelRatio)`, capped at 3. Under F10 the camera zoom already includes the pixel ratio, so both helpers use the same function.
  - `ttFit.ts`: `ttFit`. It drops the lowest-priority segments first, then shrinks, never below the role minimum. Only after that does it truncate with an ellipsis. It remembers the fit spec.
  - `healing.ts`: `installFontHealing(game)`. On `loadingdone` it re-measures and refits the registered texts, then emits `tt:fonts-healed` so a scene can re-run its `layoutHud()`.
- ESLint (`client/eslint.config.mjs`):
  - `no-restricted-imports` errors on `phaser` and `components/Phaser/**` inside `components/typography/**` and `design/**`.
  - A second instance of `no-restricted-syntax`, named `tt/no-raw-font`, warns on `fontFamily`, `font:`, `setFontFamily()`, `setFont()` and `ctx.font =`. It skips `components/typography/`, `components/Phaser/typography/`, `design/`, `tailwind.config.ts` and the typography tests.
  - Task 7b flips `tt/no-raw-font` to error once 3e has migrated the call sites.
