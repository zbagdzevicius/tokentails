# 5d: Catnip Heist first run (G10 "Heist", decision #72)

Founder item 8 (first-run onboarding), Heist bullet of G10: a brief card per first visit per level,
ghost-paw route hints, "Rewind 5 s" on levels 1-3, consent-gated first-run analytics. Nothing
committed, deployed or uploaded. The sim (`catnip-heist/src/sim/**`) and its vendored backend copy
are unchanged (checked by hash, below).

## What was built

### Pure modules, `catnip-heist/src/onboarding/` (vitest, no DOM)
- `ftue-store.ts`: `FtueStore`, one versioned JSON value under `catnip-heist.ftue.v1` in
  localStorage (`{v:1, briefs:[levelId], routeTaps, rewinds}`). Every read and write is in
  try/catch; with blocked storage it keeps the answers in memory for the page. Bad or old data
  starts fresh.
- `brief.ts`: `briefFor(level)` builds the brief card from the level's own meta: "Heist N of 8",
  the level name, `meta.intro`, the plan (the objective lines with `{cat}` filled in, at most 5),
  a controls row on levels 1-2 and tips ("Stuck? Tap the objective for a route.", plus "Seen by a
  dog? Rewind 5 s and try again." on levels 1-3). A new level gets a brief with no extra copy.
- `route.ts`: `buildRoute(level, solution)` replays the level's `.solution.json` through the real
  `stepSim` and cuts it into legs. A new leg starts when the objective stage changes (the same
  stage the objective chip shows), when the solution swaps cats, or on a respawn. A leg keeps the
  walking cat's tiles (8-connected, deduplicated) and the spots where the plan presses ACT or MEOW.
  `pickLeg(legs, level, state)` chooses the leg of the current stage, preferring the cat the
  player controls and the leg passing closest to that cat; `needsSwap` when the leg belongs to the
  parked cat. `routeGeometry` turns a pick into paw prints (alternating left and right paws,
  toes along the walk, at most 48) and marks (act, meow, swap, goal).
- `hint-tracker.ts`: `RouteHintTracker`, counted in sim ticks (pause, background tabs and the
  brief never count): the route is offered after 600 ticks (20 s) without progress or after two
  detections on one objective; a tap shows or hides it at once. Progress is a stage increase or a
  door opening, key, rescue or checkpoint event. When the stage moves on while the route is up, the
  tracker reports `done`.
- `rewind.ts`: `REWIND_TICKS = 150` (5 s), `REWIND_LEVELS` = the first three campaign levels,
  `rewindTarget(spottedTick)`, `REWIND_OFFER_MS = 6000`.
- `ghost-paws.ts` (three.js, draw only): two instanced meshes, so the hint costs two draw calls.
  Prints are an 18x18 pixel paw with a dark one-pixel rim (nearest filtering, additive), tinted
  gold with HDR colours so a pulse that walks along the prints passes the bloom threshold on the
  high tier and clamps to full brightness on the low tier. Rings mark ACT (gold), MEOW (pink), a
  swap (lilac) and the leg's goal. Prints far along the leg are dimmer. Fades in and out; static
  under reduced motion. It never moves a cat.

### Session: `catnip-heist/src/app/session.ts`
- `HeistSession.rewindTo(tick)` (play mode only): cuts `inputs` back to `tick` and re-simulates
  the cut log with `stepSim` from the nearest saved state (a keyframe every 150 ticks; states are
  immutable and come from the same `stepSim` calls, so this is the exact state a replay from
  tick 0 reaches). `log()` afterwards is the cut log plus whatever is played next, an ordinary
  input log. `rewinds` counts them.

### App and UI
- `src/app/App.ts`:
  - Brief gate: a play-mode run on a level whose brief this device has not seen opens on the brief
    card. The sim clock does not run while it is open (`gated`), pause and blur do nothing, and
    the key or tap that closes it is consumed (input is reset), so the cat never jumps. Closing it
    marks the brief seen. Replays never show it.
  - Route: per tick `RouteHintTracker.observe`; on `show` the ghost paws are drawn for the picked
    leg and redrawn when the walking cat's tile, the other cat's tile or the stage changes; on
    `done` they hide. Levels already won offer the route only on a tap (decision below).
  - Rewind: a `SPOTTED` event on levels 1-3 offers "Rewind 5 s" for 6 s (button or R). It calls
    `rewindTo(max(0, spottedTick - 150))`, resets input and the hint tracker (keeping the route on
    if it was), redraws, and toasts "Rewound 5 s". The renderer already treats a lower tick as a
    restart (`resetRun`) and rebuilds props, coins and the rescued cat from the state.
  - QA hooks (`src/app/qa.ts`, QA builds only): `briefOpen`, `closeBrief`, `ftue`, `resetFtue`,
    `route`, `tapObjective`, `rewind`, `feed(runs)`. `qaStep` and `qaFeed` close an open brief
    (the player started) and feed the first-run tracker, so `playSolution` in the client e2e
    still reaches Results.
- `src/ui/ftue.ts` (new): the brief card (night glass panel, display font title, Nunito body, plan
  list with gold step numbers, controls, tips, a gold "Go!" with focus; Enter, Space, any move key,
  Escape or a tap on the backdrop closes it; two columns on short landscape screens) and the
  "Rewind 5 s" button (sky pill with an R key cap and a 6 s drain bar, placed above the touch pad on
  touch layouts). Own `<style>` tag.
- `src/ui/ui.ts` and `src/ui/styles.ts`: the objective chip is now a button with a "Route" pill;
  tap or H toggles the route. While it is on the chip has a gold ring and its label reads "Follow
  the paw prints" or "Swap cats, then follow the paws". The zone hint and toasts hide under the
  brief.

### Analytics, `src/analytics/index.ts`
- `HEIST_RUN_EVENTS`: `ftue_gate_shown {gate}`, `game_start {is_restart}`, `ftue_hint_shown` and
  `ftue_hint_done {hint: route|rewind, trigger: idle|fails|tap|spotted}`, `life_lost {spotted,
  rewind_offered}`, `game_fail {reason: retry|menu|levels|exit}`, `game_finish`, and
  `ftue_first_clear {duration_s, elapsed_s, spotted, rewinds, route_shown}`, all with
  `mode: 'heist'` and `level`. End events carry `duration_s` (sim time), `score`, `catnip`,
  `spotted`, `rewinds`, `stars`.
- `HEIST_SENDABLE_EVENTS = HEIST_APP_EVENTS + HEIST_RUN_EVENTS`, filtered through the F9 catalog.
  The consent rule is unchanged: each send reads `tt-analytics-consent` and needs `granted`.
- Build gate: `App` imports the analytics types only. The instance comes from `main.ts`'s
  `installAnalytics()`, which is dead code in `VITE_HEIST_BUILD=replay|verify` builds, so the run
  funnel is compiled out there with the rest of analytics (a vitest checks both).

## Decisions applied (record for the final report)
1. **#72 Rewind 5 s on levels 1-3: yes, the plan's recommendation.** Done by re-simulating a cut
   input log; the sim, `verifyRun` and the sim hashes are unchanged.
2. **A rewind undoes the detection it rewinds past.** `spottedCount` goes back with the state, so
   the "Clean and quick" star is still possible after a rewind. This is consistent with the server:
   `verifyRun` computes stars from the saved log, which no longer contains that detection.
3. **The route only offers itself on levels not yet won**; a tap (or H) shows it on any level.
   Players replaying a cleared level for stars are not nagged.
4. **The brief holds the sim clock** (it is the run's start gate, like G10's `RunGate` for the
   Phaser modes), so reading it never costs par time or score.
5. **The run funnel is allow-listed in `src/analytics`** instead of editing `HEIST_APP_EVENTS` in
   `shared/analytics-core.ts` (not mine). Request to the shared-contracts owner: add the eight run
   events to `HEIST_APP_EVENTS` and drop `HEIST_RUN_EVENTS` here.
6. **Embedded first-time path is now four taps to moving** (PLAY, CATNIP HEIST, START, Go!). The
   HUD is on screen after three taps (the client `heist-host.spec` check still holds), and the
   brief closes on any move key. Kept because G10 asks for the brief on every first visit.

## Section 6.1 target (Heist-01)
- Target: `ftue_first_clear` with `mode = heist` and `level = heist-01` in session 1 for **at
  least 70%** of consenting sessions that start heist-01, **median at most 4 min**.
- Measure: funnel `ftue_gate_shown {level: heist-01}` (or `game_start {level: heist-01,
  is_restart: false}` for players who saw the brief earlier) to `ftue_first_clear {level:
  heist-01}`. Time: `elapsed_s` on `ftue_first_clear` is seconds since the Heist page loaded;
  `duration_s` is the winning run's sim time. Diagnostics: `ftue_hint_shown {hint: route}` by
  `trigger`, `life_lost` per run, `ftue_hint_done {hint: rewind}` (rewinds used), and `game_fail`
  by `reason`.
- Not measurable until a PostHog key is built in (`VITE_POSTHOG_KEY`) and the dashboards exist
  (W4b), and only over consenting sessions.

## Known limitations
- The route is the planned solution's route. It ignores guard timing (it shows where to go, not
  when) and, for a player far off the plan, starts from the nearest point of the leg.
- When the rewound tick is itself an event tick, that tick's HUD feedback (a coin bump, say) can
  play once more. Cosmetic.
- `ftue_first_clear` means the first win in the local progress store (which, embedded, includes
  the account progress the host merged in).
- The brief is not reachable again from pause (no "replay brief" button yet).

## Review fixes (second pass)
Eight review findings; all verified and fixed (none rejected). Sim, vendored copy and
`*.solution.json` untouched.

1. **Brief Go! clipped at 844x390 (high).** Confirmed. `src/ui/ftue.ts`: the card now has two
   column wrappers (`.ch-brief-col`, `display: contents` in portrait with CSS `order` keeping the
   stack kicker, title, intro, plan, controls, tips, Go!). On short landscape screens the left
   column is the story, controls and tips and the right column is the plan with Go! at its foot,
   with smaller type (tips 13px). Base card: `max-height: 100%; overflow-y: auto`. In portrait,
   Go! is `position: sticky` at the card's foot, so a long plan scrolls under it (360x640). New
   e2e at 844x390, 667x375, 360x640 and 390x844 checks that Go! sits inside the card, the card
   fits the viewport, and nothing scrolls sideways.
2. **R while paused threw the offer away (medium).** Confirmed. `onRewind` now returns a boolean
   (`App.rewind()` already did). The overlay hides the offer only when the rewind happened and
   only swallows R then. The e2e pauses, presses R, checks the tick and that the offer is still up,
   resumes, and rewinds.
3. **Rewind funnel skew (medium).** Confirmed. Choice: **once per run for both.** `RewindFunnel`
   (`src/onboarding/rewind.ts`) sends `ftue_hint_shown {hint:'rewind'}` on the first offer and
   `ftue_hint_done {hint:'rewind'}` on the first rewind of the run, so done/shown is the share of
   runs offered a rewind that used it. The rewind count stays on `ftue_first_clear` /
   `game_finish` (`rewinds`). Unit tested.
4. **e2e pinned to old sim values (medium).** Confirmed. `e2e/rewind-plan.ts` finds the detection
   at test time from the current level and solution JSON (the same search as `rewind.test.ts`).
   It computes the rewound tick and the hash a fresh Node session reaches on the cut log, so the
   page's rewind is checked against an independent replay. It restates `REWIND_TICKS` because
   `src/onboarding/rewind.ts` imports the level registry (JSON imports Node ESM cannot load in the
   Playwright runner).
5. **Chip name hid the objective (low, a11y).** Confirmed. No `aria-label`: the button is named
   by its content (label and current objective). The action ("Tap to show a route." / "Route
   shown; tap to hide it.") is a hidden span used as `aria-describedby`. A phone e2e checks the
   name and the description.
6. **Objective text against the Route pill at 390 px (low).** Confirmed. `padding-right` is now
   108px. The phone e2e checks at least 8px between the text and the pill (measured: 24px).
7. **Ghost paws faint on phones (low).** Confirmed. `src/onboarding/ghost-paws.ts` uses normal
   blending instead of additive, so the dark rim shows and the prints read on light tiles. On
   `(pointer: coarse), (max-width: 600px), (max-height: 480px)` prints are 0.7 (was 0.56) at 0.95
   opacity. Desktop is 0.85 opacity. Checked at 1280x720 and 390x844.
8. **Orphan `build/qa-*.js` served publicly (low).** Confirmed. `installQA` returns early
   `if (!QA_BUILD)`, so in production builds the chunk Rolldown still emits for main.ts's dead
   `import()` is a 70-byte stub (`function e(){return null}`), checked with a scratch production
   build. `vite.config.ts` and `main.ts` are not mine. Request to their owner: drop the chunk
   outright with a `QA_BUILD`-gated `import()` stub or a Rolldown plugin. Nothing referenced
   `client/public/heist-game/build/qa-ciE7-wVO.js`, so it was deleted from the shipped copy.
