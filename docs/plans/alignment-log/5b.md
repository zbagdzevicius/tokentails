## Decisions applied

- **#96**: Cupid Cat level 1 with the starter shield is the first mode after Meet your cat. The
  hand-off is 4a's (`components/onboarding/handoff.ts`); 5b routes a player with no Cupid clears
  straight to day 1's full gate.
- **Starter shield** (plan G10): uncleared levels 1 and 2, one hit, visible (sprite on the cat,
  HUD chip, first-seen line). After a win on the level, PLAY AGAIN runs without it.
- **Shield grace 1 s**: the old 100 ms let the same saw or spike strip take the next life a frame
  later, so the shield saved nothing. Spikes also bounce the cat up when the shield breaks.
- **Tutorial once per level per player** with "Replay tutorial", versioned key
  (`CUPID_TUTORIAL_VERSION`) so a rewrite can show it again.
- **First-seen hints** are in-world plates on the `hint` role (decision #86, sentence case), shown
  once per player; not routed through `RUN_HINT`, so `RunHint` does not draw a second copy.
- **Unlock rule** `unlocked(i) = i === 0 || cleared[i-1]` (G10 backend) on `seasonEventCleared`,
  grandfathering `best > 0` while the server field is absent. The February calendar gate stays as
  a separate check with its own toast.
- **Leaving mid-run saves nothing** (no `GAME_STOP`, so no `/live`); the plan's `quit` outcome is
  not sent by Cupid.

## Bug found and fixed along the way

- Leaving a level and opening it again threw `Cannot read properties of null (reading
  'activePointer')` in dev and blocked the respawn: `CupidHud` received the Phaser scene as a prop,
  and React 19's dev render logging walks object props, hitting the destroyed scene's input
  getters. The HUD now takes a callback. Caught by the new mid-run close step in the e2e.
