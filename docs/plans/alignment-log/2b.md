## Decisions I made (not in the plan; flag if wrong)

- **House-zone cats are not counted as rescued cats (review round 2, founder to confirm).** The public
  `rescueCats` figure and the traction counts leave out blessings in Token Tails' own zones
  (`token-tails` "catfluencers", `token-tails-2` "EVENT"; `home` is the portrait shelter), so they agree
  with the shelter list, which already leaves those zones out. `kind` stays a two-value field as the
  plan sets it (rescue or portrait): house-zone blessings are still `rescue` records (the G3 featured
  cats and packs draw from catfluencers by `kind: 'rescue'`, as the plan says), and the exclusion is by
  the shelter's `role`, in one place. The blessing backfill therefore still marks them `rescue`, and its
  dry run prints how many planned rescue rows sit in house zones. If the founder decides catfluencers
  cats are rescues, give that shelter `role: 'partner'` and both figures include it.
- **A gift fails as `timeout` only after 30 minutes AND once its nonce is used (review round 3, founder
  to confirm).** The task text says "FAILED after 30 min". The reconcile job waits 30 minutes, then
  fails a SENT gift without a receipt only when the hot wallet's mined nonce is past the gift's nonce
  (so it can never be mined later). An unused nonce keeps the gift SENT (manual step 6). Reason: failing
  it at 30 minutes alone frees the user-day and budget slots while the transaction can still be mined,
  which would pay twice.
- **Heist saves count as saved games for the instant-treat policy (review round 3; plan F7.5 applied,
  earlier exclusion reverted).** F7.5 gives one rule ("at least 1 saved game") to both treat sources,
  `page` and `heist`, and a Heist save is replay-verified on `/live`, so a Heist-only player can send
  the win-screen treat. The daily-paw rule (`paws.service.ts`, task 4f) is unchanged.
- **No automatic cancel transaction.** A gift whose nonce stays unused is kept, not replaced with a
  0-value transaction at the same nonce: that would be a chain transaction from the reconcile job.

- **Crons are opt-in outside production (`IMPACT_JOBS_ENABLED`, default on only when
  `NODE_ENV=production`; review fix).** The dev backend on :3005 is connected to a
  production-size database (541,953 registered users in `GET /impact`), so an always-on snapshot or
  reconcile would write into it from a developer machine. Before the gate went in (about 22:39 to
  22:58 EEST on 2026-09-30) the dev server's reconcile and indexer crons took leases in `jobruns`
  (documents `shelter-donate-reconcile`, `impact-indexer`) in that database. Nothing else could have
  been written: donations are paused (no PENDING or SENT rows can exist), the indexer is idle
  (`not-deployed`) and the hourly snapshot did not fire in that window. The two lease documents are
  harmless; delete them if that database is production.
- `DONATE_NOT_ELIGIBLE` (403, with `reason` and `eligibleAt`) for a verified account that is too new
  or has no saved game. It is not in F5.6; see requests below.
- Live-build cache: before the first stored snapshot, `GET /impact` builds live and caches it for
  10 minutes (it counts users); a stored row is cached for 1 minute.

  twice around the nonce lookup). A nonce is used once, so a timed-out gift can never be mined later and
  giving its slots back can never lead to two paid gifts. The hash, nonce and sender are stored before
  the broadcast (sign, store, broadcast), which also closes the "process dies between broadcast and
  writing the hash" gap: such a row is PENDING with a hash and is settled like SENT. An ambiguous
  broadcast error (timeout, lost connection) keeps the gift SENT with its slots; only a certain refusal

  `CALL_EXCEPTION`) or a failure before signing gives the slots back at once. Still open: a gift whose

  user's day until a later gift uses the nonce; and a legacy SENT row without a stored nonce that the
  node no longer knows is kept for a manual check (none exist: donations were never enabled).
