Registered in the one `AppModule` (controller, `RescueGoalStore`, `RescueGoalPledgeService`, `RescueGoalService`, five models).

**Give order of checks:** guest refused first (403 `GUEST_FORBIDDEN` from AppAuthGuard, repeated in the service, F5.4) → kill switch → replay by client UUID → F7.5 policy (registered, >= 72 h, >= 3 saved games) → balance, room, daily cap pre-checks → saga.

**Saga** (default; `MONGO_TRANSACTIONS=true` runs steps 2-5 in one transaction):
1. insert the give PENDING, unique on `(user, clientId)`: the same pledge id never debits twice;
2. reserve the daily cap (`PLEDGE_DAILY_CAP` = 5,000 from shared caps), conditional on the room left;
3. guarded debit `{tails: {$gte: n}}`, `$inc` `tails -n`, `tailsGiven +n`, `monthTailsGiven +n`; `tailsEarned` untouched, so giving never costs rank;
4. one pipeline update on the goal with `$expr raisedTails + n <= targetTails`: never overfills, and sets `FILLED` and `filledAt` in the same write;
5. CONFIRMED, holds released, `goalsHelped`/`monthGoalsHelped` +1 on a player's first give to that goal.

Each step records the give's id in the document it changes, so a repeat is a no-op. A refusal in steps 2-4 gives back what earlier steps took (REJECTED).

**Sweeper** (F8, `runLeased`, every minute, `RESCUE_GOAL_SWEEPER=off` stops it): a PENDING give whose 60 s deadline is 60 s behind is fenced on the goal, then rolled forward (the goal already counted it) or refunded. Worst case is about 3 minutes, inside the plan's 10. It also settles final gives with unreleased holds, rechecks refunded gives once after 10 minutes for a late write, and re-runs cancel refunds for 15 minutes.

**Goals:** a goal opens only with `fundingSetAside: true`, a funding line and an amount (decision #37). There is no draft state. At most 10 goals per budget month, and no house zones. Funding, proof owner and saga fields never appear in a public view, so the internal Tails-per-euro ratio can't be derived publicly. Deliver needs a photo (re-encoded WebP with metadata stripped, then public) and a receipt (private in `rescuegoalreceipts`; its SHA-256 is public). Cancel refunds every give exactly once.

**Treat Giver** (G5 "Treats"): computed on read from CONFIRMED `ShelterDonation`s this season, five needed. Cosmetic; no Tails are granted for treats. It is returned in `GET /rescue-goals/pledges/me` → `badges`.

**Opening gate:** gives answer 503 `PLEDGES_PAUSED` until `TAILS_EARNED_BACKFILL_DONE=true` (4e manual step 3), because the backfill refuses once a pledge exists and the board must sort on `tailsEarned` first. `RESCUE_GOAL_PLEDGES=off` is a kill switch.

### Script: `backend/scripts/rescue-goal-audit.js`

A read-only ledger audit. It checks that `raisedTails` equals confirmed plus counted gives, that `pledgeCount` matches, that no goal is overfilled, that FILLED and OPEN goals sit on the right side of the target, and that funded statuses have money set aside. It also flags gives PENDING for more than 10 minutes, unsettled final gives, day reservations above the cap, and stale user holds. It prints counts and goal ids only, and exits 1 on a problem so it can run as a monitor.

## Decisions applied

- **#37** (goal budget): a goal opens only when its money is marked set aside; there is no draft. One budget month holds 4 to 10 goals: 10 is enforced and 4 is shown as a hint. A named proof owner is required. The funding amount and line are CMS-only. The roughly 1,000 Tails per €1 ratio is never stored, computed or shown; managers pick the Tails target themselves.
- **#44** (pledge cap and sybil gates): 5,000 Tails a day per user, with an account at least 72 h old and at least 3 saved games (F7.5 counts saved `/live` games; "3 sessions" is read as 3 saved games, the plan's own F7.5 wording). P4: a per-user throttle on the give route.
- **Saga by default, transactions behind `MONGO_TRANSACTIONS=true`**: the W0 audit of whether Atlas runs a replica set is still deferred.
- **Give range** 10 to 5,000 Tails per give; the 100 / 1,000 / MAX chips fit inside it.
- **Cancelling a goal gives back that day's cap share (review fix 9).** `refundCancelledGoal` releases the day reservation together with the balance, so a player whose goal was cancelled can give those Tails again the same day. Reason: the cap limits Tails that actually go to goals (decision #44 is about how much a player can give, not how many times they press the button), and a cancel is the manager's decision, not the player's. A cancel cannot be triggered by players, so it is not a way around the cap.
- **Expired goals (review fix 1).** An OPEN goal past `endsAt` stays OPEN in storage (no job flips it), refuses gives with 409 `GOAL_NOT_OPEN`, is flagged `expired: true` in every view, and is left out of the current and open lists and of the open count. A manager delivers it (the money is set aside anyway), cancels it (every give goes back), or extends `endsAt` in "Edit wording", which reopens it. The CMS shows an "Ended, needs action" chip and a notice.
- **Cover pictures (review fix 6)** must be on Token Tails storage: the `DO_SPACES_CDN` host (what the CMS image upload returns) or the two public Spaces buckets already used for cat and shelter art (`GOAL_IMAGE_HOSTS`). Any other https host is a 400.

## Review fixes (second pass)

All nine review findings were valid; each is fixed or recorded.

1. **Expired goals stayed OPEN in lists and counts.** `list()` leaves expired OPEN goals out of `current` and `open`; `openGoalsSummary` counts `{status: OPEN, $or: [{endsAt: null}, {endsAt: {$gt: now}}]}`; `PublicGoal`/`ManagerGoal` carry `expired`; the CMS chip and notice tell managers to deliver or cancel. Specs: in memory (list, summary, views, cancel still works) and on real MongoDB (summary count).
2. **Parallel deliveries could leave a receipt that did not match the public hash.** `deliver()` now turns the goal DELIVERED first (status-guarded), and only the winner stores its receipt. If the receipt write fails, the delivery is undone (back to FILLED or OPEN) and the manager gets 503 to retry. A delivery that loses to a cancel stores nothing. Specs: two parallel deliveries in memory, three on real MongoDB, a delivery racing a cancel, and a failed receipt write followed by a retry.
3. **The sweeper could release the cap share of a give its request had confirmed.** `recover()` only undoes holds and sets `recheckAt` when its own `PENDING -> REFUNDED` write matched. Otherwise it reloads the give, settles it if it is CONFIRMED and unsettled, lifts the fence, and reports `forwarded` (or skips a give the request already refused). Spec in memory and on real MongoDB: the day still holds 400 and no recheck is set.
4. **`reject()` undid unconditionally.** Same `modifiedCount` guard: a give the sweeper already refunded is left to it. Spec added.
5. **The route test did not go through Nest or Express.** New `rescue-goal-http.spec.ts` starts a real Nest app (ShelterController and RescueGoalController in AppModule order, services stubbed, a stand-in `appauth` passport strategy) and sends HTTP requests with `fetch`: `GET /shelter/:id` reaches ShelterController, `GET /rescue-goals/pledges/me` reaches `myGives` and not `:id`, `admin/goals` reaches the manager list (403 for players), guests get 403 and no token 401 before the pledge handler. No new dependency (no supertest).
6. **Any https cover picture was accepted.** Allowlisted hosts (see Decisions). Spec added.
7. **No server MIME or size check on the delivery photo.** 400 unless JPEG, PNG or WebP, and 400 above 5 MB, both before decoding or uploading. The CMS checks the same 5 MB limit before upload. Spec added.
8. **CMS accessibility.** The status filter is a button group (`role="group"`, `aria-pressed`) instead of tabs without the tab pattern. "Still needed: ..." is plain text tied to the submit button with `aria-describedby`, not a `role="status"` live region that re-announced on every keystroke. Failures still arrive through the toast.
9. **A cancel hands back that day's cap share.** Kept and recorded as a decision above.

Also fixed: a CMS test raced the browser SHA-256 of the receipt under a loaded full run (about 1 in 3). It now waits for the hash with `findByTestId`.

## Known limitations

- Day documents expire by TTL 8 days after their day. The audit only checks live reservations.
- The receipt is stored in Mongo (up to 10 MB per goal), not in the bucket, so it never becomes public by mistake. Move it to private object storage if receipt volume grows.
- The delivery photo upload needs `DO_SPACES_*` on the instance; without it, deliver answers 503 and nothing is written.
- No known-issue entry was fixed as a side effect.
- Nothing flips an expired goal's status; it waits for a manager. The audit script does not flag expired goals yet.
- The receipt write and the goal update are two writes. If the process dies between them, the goal shows DELIVERED with no stored receipt (its receipt download answers 404); undoing that needs a manual step. With `MONGO_TRANSACTIONS=true` this could move into a transaction later.
