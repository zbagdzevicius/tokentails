IMPACT (default), REWARDS, MISSIONS, TIERS, PET ART, BADGES and VAULT.

- **Tabs.** IMPACT is the default. The tab comes from the `tab` prop, else the lobby's pending
  request (`takeProgressTab()`, task 5e), and a `tt:progress-tab` event switches an open PROGRESS.
  Closing PROGRESS calls `clearProgressTab()`. PET ART keeps the app-build filter. VAULT renders
  only on the web when `GET /user/token-status` says TOKEN. App builds never call it, and any
  failure (network, non-2xx, a bad body) means POINTS.
- **Season band.** It reads `season` from `GET /user/airdrop/progression` and shows `freezeAt`
  (22:00 UTC on the 8th) in the viewer's local time with the time left. During the freeze it shows
  when the next season starts (`anchorAt`). `TGE_TARGET_DATE`, `getNextMonthStartUtc`, the TGE
  COUNTDOWN, the NEXT MONTH COUNTDOWN and the client-computed "9th" are deleted. The badge reset
  wait reads the season instead.
- **Rescue Goal card.** It shows the first OPEN goal that is not expired (`GET /rescue-goals`, with
  `?surface=app` on app builds). Progress is in Tails with the IN-GAME chip, and there are no money
  figures and no giver counts. The 100, 1,000 and MAX chips are px-sized buttons of at least 44 px.
  MAX is the smallest of the balance, today's room under the cap, what the goal still needs and
  `limits.max`. When chips are off, a line says why: giving paused, account under 72 h, fewer
  than 3 saved games, the daily cap, or a low balance. Every give goes through the confirm sheet,
  which says "Giving never lowers your rank." and repeats the no-cash-value line. The sheet makes a
  UUID when it opens. `pledgeWithRetry` re-sends the same id after a 503 PLEDGE_INTERRUPTED, a
  timeout or a dropped connection (up to 3 tries), and "Try again" also re-sends it. A refused or
  returned give gets a new id. A guest tap asks them to save their cat (`save-progress`), and a
  guest never gives. The empty state appears when no goal is funded.
- **Treat card.** It has seven states: ready, on-its-way, sent-today, not-eligible, paused,
  budget-spent and failed. They come from the DONATE_* codes of `POST /shelter/donate` (source
  `page`, plan 2.13 #28), the caller's `GET /shelter/donate/me` row for today, and the public
  `GET /shelter/donate/status`. Next-treat times show in local time. The card says Token Tails
  pays and that treats give no Tails. The community total is `<Claim L-treats>` with its tier.
- **Today's paw.** It shows `PawProgress` (5e) with the local countdown. Its line follows
  `pawSettlements.sendEnabled`. "See the proof" opens `/impact`, or web `/impact` through
  `@capacitor/browser` on app builds.
- **MY IMPACT.** It shows paws, treats, Tails given, goals helped, Treat Giver progress, and this
  season's gives. It needs no purchase and no cat.
- **DELIVERED.** Each card has the photo, the date and the note. The web shows the receipt
  SHA-256 fingerprint. App builds show no hash: they show "Receipt checked · see the proof", which
  opens web `/impact`.
- **Events.** `impact_tab_viewed` fires once per IMPACT mount. `pledge_made` fires on a counted
  give. `treat_sent {status}` fires on every send.
- **Explainer.** The first Codex entry calls `saveNudge.firstCodexEntry()` and queues "Tails are
  rescue points". The card shows only when `explainerAllowed(scene)` is true: a menu (no mode,
  or a level picker) or a game-over panel (`gameStop`), never a running scene. `CodexModal`
  passes the scene from `useGame()`. Dismissing it stores `tt.tailsExplainer.v1` in localStorage
  (in try/catch). It is an inline card at the top of PROGRESS, not a second dialog. A dialog on
  top of PROGRESS hid PROGRESS from assistive tech (Radix `hideOthers`) and broke every other spec
  that opens the Codex.
- **Copy (G5 map).** The changes:
  - AIRDROP COMMAND CENTER becomes YOUR REWARDS.
  - "READY FOR SNAPSHOT" becomes "OF CHECKS MET".
  - "maximize your allocation" is deleted with the NEXT MONTH block.
  - The MISSIONS tip and the TIERS tag use rescue-points wording.
  - `$TAILS` becomes `formatTails`, including the claim toasts.
  - "1 badge = 300 $TAILS per month" (R8) becomes "Each badge adds 300 Tails at every season reset".
  - The FAQ shows the season start in local time.
  - The portrait flow drops "before airdrop claims".
  - Lore #3, #6 and #7 lose the token and Web3 lines, and the nap how-to uses `CAT_NAP_*`.
  - The COLLECTIBLE card shows Tails given and goals helped instead of packs and portraits.

## Review fixes (round 2)

All eight review findings were valid and are fixed.

1. Interrupted give counted twice: `pick` re-uses the interrupted draft's pledge id when the goal
   and amount match (the sheet reopens on "Try again"), and closing the sheet after an interruption
   reloads the goal and the account's gives (`closeSheet`).
2. Guest signs in from a chip: the `runWithAccount` callback always reads the account's gives
   (`fetchQuery`, 15 s stale time), recomputes `giveOptions(goal, "registered", fresh)` and, when
   that chip is off, shows the note as a toast instead of opening a sheet bound to fail.
3. After a refusal or "goal filled first" the sheet is `final`: no confirm button, "Not now"
   becomes "Close". Only `GOAL_OVERFLOW` with room left re-reads the goal and offers the smaller
   give ("You can give N Tails instead.") as a new give with a new id.
4. PROGRESS tabs: the open tab carries `aria-pressed="true"` (new optional `pressed` prop on
   `PixelButton`, outside the owns list, additive only) and a gold bar under it. On touch screens
   (`@media (hover:none)`) the sticky hover look is off; `filter-none`, not `brightness-100`,
   because any filter breaks the button art's `mix-blend-darken` and shows grey corners.
5. Chain words: BADGES "earn your first badge"; codex entry #2 "When we play, we mend" (it also
   said "mint" and shows in app builds); PET ART "Pay by card or crypto to create the portrait".
6. A link to VAULT shows "LOADING…" (`vault-loading`) while token-status is pending, then VAULT or,
   in POINTS mode, IMPACT. No jump.
7. Tests: the lobby RESCUE test checks IMPACT is open (and `aria-pressed`) after reopening; the
   running-scene test calls `window.__TT_E2E_GAME__.openModal("CODEX")`, a hook `GameProvider`
   (`context/GameContext.tsx`, outside the owns list) sets only when `__TT_E2E__` is true. RTL:
   interrupted give keeps its id and reloads gives; refusal leaves no confirm; overflow offers the
   smaller give with a new id; a guest signing in with a too-new account gets a toast, no sheet;
   tab `aria-pressed`; VAULT link waits for token-status (TOKEN and POINTS).
8. Goal card: `mt-auto` removed from the steps list; no gap at 1440.

Checks: tsc clean; eslint clean on changed files; jest full run green; the `codex-*` suites run 38 tests,
all pass; Playwright `progress-impact` at 390 and 1440: 17
passed, 3 skipped; `modals-a` + `lobby-impact` at 390 and 1440: 41 passed, 2 failed under 3
workers (Wheel redeem timeout, lobby 404 crawl), both pass when re-run alone (dev-server load).

## Review fixes (round 3)

All five findings were valid. Four are code fixes; the fifth is documented.

1. Treat card stuck on "on its way": `treatState` now lets a read saying today's treat is
   `CONFIRMED` override a `sent` result (`sent-today`). Unit test: "sent, then the read says
   CONFIRMED, gives sent-today" (`codex-impact-logic`).
2. Open tab hard to see: the open tab gets a gold ring, a gold tint and a glow, plus a
   `h-1.5 inset-x-2` gold bar. The other tabs are dimmed (`opacity-70 saturate-50`) and only lift
   on hover or `focus-visible`. On touch screens the sticky hover is off.
3. Dialog title: `CodexModal` is titled PROGRESS. `modals-a.spec.ts` and `progress-impact.spec.ts`
   look for the dialog named PROGRESS. The modal id `CODEX` (`openModal("CODEX")`, golden-path's
   modal list) is unchanged.
4. MAX after an interrupted give: when the last give was interrupted (`canRetry`) on the same
   goal, any chip, MAX included, reopens the sheet with that give's amount and pledge id. The
   backend replays it if it went through, so a smaller MAX never becomes a second give by
   accident. RTL test in `codex-impact-tab`.
5. Explainer surface (departure from the spec, kept): the explainer shows only on the PROGRESS
   surface. The request is made only when PROGRESS opens (`Codex.tsx`), and no HUD control opens
   PROGRESS during a running scene, so in real play the request is always made on a menu and
   shows at once. A request that waits (only reachable through the E2E hook) shows the next time
   PROGRESS is opened on a menu or a game-over screen. It is not mounted in the lobby or the
   end-of-run panels, to keep those screens unchanged.
