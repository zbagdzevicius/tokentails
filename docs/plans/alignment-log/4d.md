### Changes outside my files (minimal, reported)

1. **`client/components/ui/GameModal.tsx` (1c):** a real bug. The title row had `px-4 … md:px-6` plus `pr-14 lg:pr-20` for the X, but `md:px-6` overrides `pr-14` between md and lg. At 844x390 the X covered the end of the title (e2e measured 20 px of overlap). The row now uses `pl-4 md:pl-6`, plus `pr-[56px] lg:pr-[80px]` when dismissible and `pr-4 md:pr-6` otherwise. ## Match3 header reserve: the contract for task 5c

- The play wrapper sets `--tt-header-reserve-right: calc(56px + var(--tt-close-grow, 0px) + env(safe-area-inset-right))`. `lg:[--tt-close-grow:20px]` adds 20 px at `lg`, where the X grows to 64 px.
- The variable inherits down to the canvas. `registerHeaderReserveProperty()` registers it as a typed `<length>` (`CSS.registerProperty`), so it computes to plain px, for example `"56px"`. Without the registration, `getComputedStyle` would return the raw `calc()` tokens.
- How the scene reads it: `parseFloat(getComputedStyle(this.game.canvas).getPropertyValue("--tt-header-reserve-right"))`.
- Also present: a `data-header-reserve-right` attribute and the `headerReservePx` prop (default 56).
- What `hudLayout.ts` already does: it reserves `CLOSE_BUTTON_RESERVE = 56` on each side of the title plate below 600 px width.
- **Still open for 5c**, measured by the e2e:
  - With a 47 px top safe area (390 project), the X sits at y 47 to 91 and covers the right stat card ("SCORE"), because the scene ignores the top safe area. The title plate also sits under the notch.
  - On landscape 844x390 (`smallLandscape`), the right stack sits under the X at y 8 to 52.
  - The e2e marks both cases `fixme` (annotation `pending-5c`) only while `Match3Scene.ts` does not reference the reserve variable. Once 5c reads it, the same test asserts no overlap.

## Decisions applied

- **End-of-run panels:** `panel` surface, `size="lg"`. At `md` (512 px) "PLAYED FOR 37 SECONDS" wrapped. Backdrop tap still closes them, as before.
- **Paw Match X:** `viewport` placement (`sticky` was the other option). The X must stay put over a full-screen canvas, and only `viewport` applies safe-area insets (F3.4).
- **TailsCardModal and VideoPlayer use `art`:** the card or video is the art, so the X hangs `outside`.
- **Picker, payment, card and video use `layer="modal-nested"`:** they open over another modal (Codex, Packs, Cats) or used to sit above it (`z-[120]`).
- **The video is skippable** (X and Esc). Before, it could not be dismissed.
- **End-of-run panel pauses the game behind it (behaviour change, recorded):** `EndGamePanel` is a GameModal with the default `suspendGame=true`, so while it is open the game loop sleeps and playing sounds pause, including any jingle or outro the scene plays after GAME_STOP. The old overlay did neither. Kept, because a modal with a running canvas behind it fights keyboard focus and screen readers. If a fanfare must keep playing, pass `suspendGame={false}` to the end-game GameModal.
- **Copy kept as is:** TailsCardModal "CRAFT … $TAILS" and MysteryBoxCat "WIN $TAILS" are G5 copy work (W5). The end-panel wording belongs to 5e.

## Known bug fixed as a side effect

- **PLAY AGAIN passed the click event as `nextLevel`:** `onClick={tryAgain}` handed the React event to `tryAgain(nextLevel?)`. It is now `onClick={() => tryAgain()}`, so PLAY AGAIN retries the same level. Covered by `modals-b-end-game.test.tsx` (`toHaveBeenCalledWith()`).
