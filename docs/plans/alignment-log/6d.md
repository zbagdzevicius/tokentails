## What was built

### Palette and art bible (G7 bullet 1)

`client/scripts/art/palette.mjs` writes `client/art/palette.json` and `client/art/palette.gpl` (GIMP,
Aseprite, Krita swatches):

- `tokens`: every colour in `client/design/tokens.ts`, loaded the way `build-tokens.mjs` loads it.
- `hero`: k-means (k = 20, seeded) over `hero-bg` and `hero-ground` in OKLab; 9 chromatic hues
  become 9-step ramps whose shadows lean violet and lights lean gold, as the hero is lit.
- `bridges`: the hero is violet and ember only, so hue gaps over 60 degrees (grass, water, ice) get
  calm ramps (hues 125, 173, 220). Without them green tiles snapped to violet.
- `sprig`: the G8 catnip colours. `neutral`: a 12-step violet-grey ramp.
- `looks.<set>`: one palette pass per plate set (#50): a 12-step ramp at the set hue and 24 sky shades
  clustered from the hero sky after that set's grade.
- `rules`: collidable and hazard contrast 3:1, plate edge-SSIM 0.85, palettes hard for plates and
  tiles, at most 256 colours per look (largest is about 190, so every output is an indexed PNG).

Hazards keep their exact silhouette (alpha mask unchanged) and get their own warm, bright grade;
collidables keep 3:1 against their plate band (the gate below).

### The build (G7 bullet 2): `client/scripts/art/build.mjs`

`node scripts/art/build.mjs` writes everything; `--gates [--json]` rebuilds in memory and only checks;
`--preview DIR` also writes contact sheets and scene previews. The build is deterministic (two runs gave
identical bytes for every output).

- **Source is the runtime sheet**, not `-original.png`: four authored sheets (summer, camp, sei,
  cat-winter) differ from the shipped `base/<sheet>.png`. The runtime sheet is un-extruded (the round
  trip un-extrude, extrude reproduces all 13 shipped sheets pixel for pixel), graded per pixel in OKLab,
  snapped to the look palette, and re-extruded to the exact runtime grid (32 x 32, margin 1, spacing 2;
  the profile `tile-extruder --margin 0 --spacing 0` produced).
- **Catnip tile** (gid 248) is redrawn as the G8 sprig (`art/catnip/catnip-32.txt`) at the same index.
- **Night value plus a lit edge** (review finding 2): fills are graded to a night value (`TILE_GRADES`:
  lightness x0.74-0.76, no lift, chroma x0.85, a cool tint), so every skin is darker than its day sheet.
  Readability comes from a 1 px lit edge (`TILE_GRADES.rim`, the fill colour lifted toward the hero's
  warm lamp light and snapped to the palette) on the sides of a collidable tile that face open air in
  the maps: `sideExposure()` in `lib/tiled.mjs` reads every placement in `blocks`, `platforms` and
  `jumper` (flip flags respected) and a side gets the edge when it is open in more than half of them
  (`RIM_OPEN_SHARE`). Interior tiles (never at an open edge) get no edge, so stacked fill shows no grid.
  The edge's lift grows in 0.04 steps per art group until it passes. Hazards keep their warm whole-cell
  grade and lift. The Shelter signs keep the old, lighter grade (`TILE_GRADES.signs`) for legibility.
- **Contrast gate from the Tiled layers**: for each sheet, the gids used in `blocks`, `platforms` and
  `jumper` by every map that loads it (Home, Shelter, the Purrsuit chapters from `map.ts`, the 14 Cupid
  days). A collidable tile is measured on its lit-edge pixels, a hazard on its whole cell, against the
  **p90** luminance of rows 40-100% of every plate set the sheet is shown on (review finding 6: it was
  the mean, so tiles over the dusk glow were never checked against it). Tiles never at an open edge are
  reported as `interior` and not measured. The catnip cell (a pickup, redrawn as the sprig) is not a
  collidable. Worst ratios now: 3.01-4.46 across all 12 sheets and 14 sets.
- **Palette validation**: every plate and sheet pixel must be in its look palette (hard).
- **Versioned names** and `public/look/manifest.json` (contract above). No existing file is overwritten.

`client/scripts/art/tile-legend.mjs` replaces `scripts/a.js` and `scripts/b.js` (deleted; known issue
"hard-coded Windows paths" fixed and recorded in `docs/CLIENT.md` and `docs/GAMES.md`):

### Parallax plates (G7 bullet 4; #46, #49, #50)

`client/scripts/art/lib/plates.mjs`, at the hero's art grid (2752 x 1536 / 4 = 688 x 384):

- far: the `hero-bg` sky; below the dark front range (found per column, median-smoothed) one flat colour,
  so parallax never shows a second copy of the range.
- mid: the `hero-bg` front range.
- near: the `hero-ground` ruins and foliage; the central altar and steps (they read as a floor) are
  replaced by the flanks' own foliage, mirror-tiled, under a ragged seeded bush line that overlaps the
  hills behind the stage and tapers into the flanks (review finding 1: cutting them out left a
  rectangular hole with 154-row vertical alpha walls and a 108-column straight bottom edge, so 6e's
  `plateCheck` rejected every near plate and drew the procedural one).
- fog: generated, a stepped-alpha band from the set ramp with a seeded wobble plus per-column jitter
  on its top edge (the smooth wobble alone left flat runs of 28-90 columns).

Each set grades these (Home: moonlit, warm horizon turned violet; Shelter: dusk, warm kept; families:
their own hue), and the horizon never rotates through yellow-green. Quantise gate: edge-SSIM (Sobel on a
3 x 3 binomial pre-blur, 8 x 8 windows, min-max normalised) between the plate and the same grade
unquantised: lowest 0.857 (`purrsuit-summer` mid), all others 0.88-0.96. Mirror-wrapped to 1376 wide.
Edge gates (all layers, in memory and on disk): the runtime's `straightCropRun` rule ported to
`lib/gates.mjs` for the lowest and the highest opaque row per column, a horizontal alpha-edge run
anywhere inside the plate (all < 28 = `minCropRun(1376)`; worst now 23, a temple roof), and a vertical
alpha-wall run (< 80 rows; worst now 66, a hero pillar). `art-pipeline.test.ts` also runs 6e's own
`straightCropRun` on every manifest plate.
Total: 56 plate files, 1.8 MB. Posters (14 webp, 0.5 MB) are the composites, for the CSS until boot.

### Capture and the landing reel (G7 bullet 5; #53)

- `client/e2e/capture/reel.capture.ts` and `playwright.capture.config.ts` (not part of the e2e
  suite: `npx playwright test -c e2e/capture/playwright.capture.config.ts`, `REEL_CLIPS=home,heist` for
  a subset). Each clip uses the shared e2e fixtures (mocked backend, network guard, fixed clock, seeded
  `Math.random`) and a fake signed-in account, boots its mode in look v1 (`tt-look-version`), pauses the
  page clock, then steps it one frame at a time at 24 fps with scripted keys on fixed frame numbers and
  a screenshot per step. With 6e's hooks (`NEXT_PUBLIC_CAPTURE=1`, `window.__TT_CAPTURE__`) it steps
  Phaser through `step()` and sets `__TT_CAPTURE_SEED__`; 60 Hz game frames per 24 fps video frame
  are 2, 3, 2, 3 with the fraction carried (`e2e/capture/frames.ts`; review finding 5: `step(2.5)` was
  floored to 2 and ran the game at 80%). Framing (review finding 3): desktop landscape 960 x 444 at
  2x with `isMobile`/`hasTouch` off; before recording every DOM element that neither is nor contains
  the game canvas (or the Heist iframe) is hidden, so no D-pad, jump or ability buttons, CLOSE,
  GO BACK/SHELTER or dev badge; frames are cropped to the canvas. A non-dry run refuses to write
  clips when the build has no hooks; `REEL_DRY_RUN=1` previews into the scratch frames directory.
  (Calling `camera.centerOn` from the driver froze the scene's rendering, so it is not used; the
  Home inputs are shorter walks either side so the cat stays in frame.)
- `client/e2e/capture/encode.ts`: WebM with no ffmpeg (MediaRecorder on a canvas, one
  `requestFrame()` per frame, bitrate from the 1.5 MB budget, retried lower if over).
- Clips: **withdrawn after the review** (finding 3). The first batch was captured on a touch phone
  profile (touch controls, CLOSE and GO BACK in every clip), on the procedural backdrop and with a
  black band in Purrsuit. Those files are deleted from `client/public/reel/` (never referenced
  outside the manifest, never shipped) and the manifest has `clips: []`. A dry run with the fixed
  driver and plates (page clock, not shippable) is in the scratch `6d-fix/fc/` (all five clips
  0.76-0.90 MB, no overlays, the new plates behind Purrsuit, no black band). `art-reel.test.tsx`
  requires every listed clip to have `steppedBy: "__TT_CAPTURE__"`. Clips are WebM (VP9) only: iOS
  Safari before 17.4 and older WKWebViews cannot play them and show the poster (finding 9); add an
  MP4 (H.264) rendition when the CDN step runs.
- The Heist clip's caption says scores save to your account when you sign in, since 4b shipped
  account-linked Heist saves (caveat copy not needed).
- `GameplayReel.tsx`: tabs per clip (tablist with arrow, Home and End keys); no `src` until the
  section is within 300 px of the viewport; `muted`, `loop`, `playsInline`, `preload="none"`; plays only
  while in view; a visible 44 px Pause/Play control (WCAG 2.2.2) in the caption row under the video
  (not on it, where it read as game UI), its label the only state signal (no `aria-pressed`, review
  finding 7); under reduced
  motion only the poster shows until the viewer presses Play; `aria-label` on each video, `alt` on

## Capture determinism

The test now skips only when the build has no hooks (`steppedBy: "page.clock"`); with the hooks a
mismatch fails through `expect(report.deterministic)` (review finding 4: it skipped on any mismatch).


`$REEL_FRAMES_DIR/determinism.json`: 0 of 16 frames byte-identical across two runs, about 0.2% of pixels
different. Cause: the dev server on :3001 has no capture hooks (`NEXT_PUBLIC_CAPTURE` unset). The clock
can only be paused after a real-time boot, so the scene's state at frame 0 differs between runs. The
determinism test then skips and logs this reason. **Manual step**: run the capture against a
`NEXT_PUBLIC_CAPTURE=1` build (`next build` with the flag into its own dist dir, or a second checkout)
so `__TT_CAPTURE__.step()` runs from game time 0. Then re-run with `-g determinism`.
