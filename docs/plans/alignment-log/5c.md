## Review round (fixes)

All six review findings were confirmed and fixed. One more defect came out of the new tests and was fixed too.

| # | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The glow and glove showed while the intro drop was still falling; the drop ignored reduced motion | `buildBoardFromTypes` returns the drop's promise. `create()` runs `finishIntro()` (tutorial, glove, nudge, first announcement) when it resolves, guarded by `introToken` so a restart cannot fire it. Swaps and idle hints wait while `introDropping` is true. Under reduced motion the tiles are placed directly (no drop, also for the board reset and reshuffle). `tt:scene-ready` stays on the first frame, so the drop fades in with the canvas. The snapshot's `cellsLanded` lets the e2e assert that every tile is on its cell while the glow shows. |
| 2 | The ghosts froze behind a paused glove | `updateGloveGhosts` runs on the scene's `update` event (removed in `stopGlove`), so ghosts fade out during `hold` and `repeatDelay`. The e2e samples the glove for 3.2 s and asserts ghost alpha `[0, 0]` whenever the glove has been still for more than 160 ms. |
| 3 | `aria-live` changed politeness on a single element; repeated messages were not re-read | New `components/Match3/SceneAnnouncer.tsx` with two fixed regions: `role="status"` polite and `role="alert"` assertive (`match3-announcer`, `match3-announcer-assertive`). Each message clears its region, then writes the text 60 ms later, so a repeat is announced again. Tested in `__test__/match3-announcer.test.ts`. |
| 4 | An idle "TRY THIS SWAP" fired as soon as the tutorial ended | `registerActivity()` runs in `completeTutorialIfNeeded()` and wherever `busy` clears after a swap or cascade (valid, special and invalid). The e2e checks there is no hint at tutorial end and none before the idle delay, measured in scene time because the page clock runs ahead of the wall clock under load. |
| 5 | Camera shake and flash ran under reduced motion | `shakeCamera()` wrapper; `cameras.main.flash` and `triggerBoardFlash` are skipped under reduced motion. The HUD lines still carry the feedback. |
| 6 | The plate could hide stats | New jest tests: the plate never covers `timer` or `score` at 360x740, 390x844 (with and without insets) and 844x390 (with and without insets); the `cards` overlay never appears at 700 px height or more. **The new test found a real bug:** in small landscape the plate covered TIME, SCORE and BEST, so "the clock waits for you" sat over a clock that was hidden. The plate now goes in the left panel between BEST and the reward line, over the combo line, which is hidden during the tutorial (`smallLandscapeTutorial` in `hudLayout.ts`). `match3-layout.test.ts` no longer lets a `panel` plate cover timer, score or best. The e2e `checkLayout` also asserts that `hiddenHud` contains neither `timer` nor `score`. |
