# Research lens: cat / pet / cozy (Oct 15, 2026)

Question: which cat, pet and cozy fantasies spread on their own, which ones make money, and which ones
web games leave unserved, for a new Token Tails web game that has to find its own audience.

Rules for this file: every number has a source link. "Unknown" means I could not find a primary
number. Aggregator and estimate sites are marked **(est.)**. Telegram titles appear here only as market
evidence. Telegram is excluded as a channel for us.

## 3. Which hooks spread, and why

1. **Cat chaos and mischief.** I Am Cat (knock things over, bother granny), Little Kitty Big City
   (steal, trip people), Roblox BAD CAT and Kitty. Real TikTok clips work the same way: "Oyen the slipper
   thief" reached 6.5M views on one video ([Malay Mail](https://malaymail.com/news/life/2025/08/09/meet-oyen-the-adorable-thief-behind-the-slipper-heist-in-his-neighbourhood/186997)).
   The cat-as-villain clip is the most repeatable shareable unit: short, physical and funny with the sound off.
2. **The "this is MY cat" identity hook.** Neko Atsume's photo album, the AI portrait apps and the
   "cat distribution system" meme ([KnowYourMeme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/cat-distribution-system))
   all rely on it. Owners post their own cat with pride, and the tagged cat pulls in friends.
3. **Meme-sound loops.** OIIA reached 2B+ views. A desktop-pet game (OiiAi Cat) and Bongo Cat (194k
   concurrent players) show a meme turns into players, **but neither turned into revenue**. Bongo Cat made
   about $2–4k/month, a warning about free idle games with weak monetization.
4. **Voice and body play that makes funny clips** (Meowgic shouting spells, "meow to revive"). The
   mechanic itself produces streamable content.
5. **Real-cat stakes.** Fresh Step × Stray "Adopt-a-Stray" made real adoptable shelter cats into in-game
   avatars for 48 hours. Results: +124% adoption applications, about 500k unique views, 11M+ earned PR
   impressions ([WARC](https://www.warc.com/en/article/fresh-step%3A-adopt-a-stray-ce5a222aeb9041afac08fd8c0107e663)).
   This is direct evidence that **real shelter cats inside a game earn press and goodwill**, and it is
   Token Tails' own differentiator.
6. **Small accounts can still go viral.** TikTok shows each video to a small test audience first and
   ignores follower count. The Matriarch went from 400 to 23k wishlists after one video hit 1M views
   ([GameDeveloper](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/from-400-to-20-000-wishlists-with-1-video-how-tiktok-can-make-your-game-go-viral)).
   Caveat: one 2025 source reports organic reach dropped sharply early in 2025
   ([presskit.gg](https://presskit.gg/field-guides/tiktok-indie-game-marketing)), so expect many attempts
   per hit. New Folder Games shipped 9 titles before I Am Cat hit.

## 4. What pet owners pay for

- They spend on their real animal and on things that represent it: 39% of the cat budget goes to
  products ([APPA](https://www.petfoodindustry.com/pet-food-market/market-trends-and-reports/news/15834866/report-cat-ownership-grows-to-53-million-us-households)).
  Custom portraits and merch of "my cat" sell on Etsy (the Token Tails Etsy plan already exists).
- Cosmetics and collectibles pay at scale in pet games: Adopt Me (est. $353M), Cats & Soup (60M
  downloads, F2P with costumes), Catizen ($26M, 1M+ payers, inflated by tokens).
- Premium "be the cat" games sell at $15–25 (I Am Cat, Little Kitty Big City), but that model is not
  recurring revenue.
- Subscription pet companions work when tied to self-care or a routine (Finch; revenue not public).
- Bongo Cat shows that a large free audience earns almost nothing without a store.
- Willingness to pay specifically for **digital items of one's own cat** in a web game: **unknown**. No
  primary data was found. /yourcat's paid HD portrait will be the first real data point.

## 5. Cat fantasies that web games do not serve

| Fantasy | Who serves it today | Web gap |
|---|---|---|
| Be a chaotic cat in a 3D home or city (physics mayhem) | I Am Cat (VR, $15), Little Kitty Big City (console/PC), Roblox BAD CAT | No polished, no-install mobile-browser 3D version. **Large gap.** |
| Play as **your own real cat** | AI portrait apps (static), /yourcat (2D pixel) | Nobody turns your cat into a playable 3D avatar that friends can see in a shared world. **Large gap. Token Tails has a head start.** |
| Help **real shelter cats** through play | Fresh Step stunt (48 h, one campaign); Token Tails | No ongoing game. **A unique story we already own.** |
| Collect and decorate (Neko Atsume, Cats & Soup) | Mobile apps, crowded | Small gap on web; not a growth engine on its own |
| Cat co-op and party play (Meowgic, Roblox roleplay) | Steam and Roblox | Medium gap: browser party rooms with shareable link invites |
| Cat heist and stealth | Catnip Heist (ours), Little Kitty Big City's stealing | We already have the engine and the theme |

## 6. Implications for the Token Tails game

- **Combine these three:** (a) chaos-cat physics clips, (b) "this is MY cat" via a /yourcat photo turned
  into a 3D or 2.5D avatar, and (c) real Pink Paw cats as named NPCs or rescue goals, with real money
  going to the shelter.
- Every session should produce a **15-second vertical replay** ("my cat destroyed grandma's kitchen in
  0:42") with the owner's cat name and a challenge link. Each clip is an ad and its link takes the viewer
  straight into the game.
- Revenue should come from cosmetics and collectibles of your own cat (hats, skins, rooms), plus HD
  portrait and merch upsells. An optional "Shelter Patron" monthly subscription can add recurring
  revenue. Bongo Cat shows that a large free audience earns almost nothing without a store.
- Plan for **many short content tests** (the New Folder Games and The Matriarch pattern), not one big
  launch.
