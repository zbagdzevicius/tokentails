# Viral game concepts: ranking (Oct 15, 2026)

Eight merged concepts, each scored 0–10 on nine factors by three independent judges (two neutral, one
adversarial). The factor scores below are the judge means. The weighted total uses these weights:

| Factor | Weight |

| Viral K-factor | 2 |
| Clipability for creators | 2 |
| UGC friendliness | 1.5 |

| Token Tails fit | 1 |
| Competition whitespace | 1 |
| Time to first traction | 1.5 |
| **Sum** | **13** |

Weighted total = Σ(score × weight) / 13. "Success" is the judges' mean probability of reaching
**10k MAU and $1k MRR within 6 months**.

How to read it:

- **The top five are close to a tie** (5.33–5.57, a 0.24 spread). Bad Cat Daily and Catnip Tower are
  only 0.01 apart. Order among them should be decided by build cost and the cheapest kill test, not by
  the scores.
- **No concept clears 10% success.** Every panel landed between 3.7% and 6.7%. The common cause is zero
  audience and a K-factor below 1 for every concept (best estimates 0.2–0.5). Growth has to come from
  many clip attempts and niche-community seeding, not from a loop compounding on its own.
- **Token Tails fit is high everywhere (7.0–8.7)** and barely separates the field. Clipability,
  feasibility and time to traction do.
- **MRR is weak across the board (3.0–5.0).** Every model needs roughly 5–50k engaged MAU to reach
  $1k MRR. Sponsors are upside, not MRR (fly.pieter went from ~$87k MRR to $0).
- **Catnip Tower is the cheapest real shot** (feasibility 7.3, time to traction 6.3, about 2–3 weeks)
  but sits in the most crowded space (Suika and cat-stack clones). **Bad Cat Daily has the best fantasy
  and whitespace** but a 4–8 week physics build.

## Concepts

### 1. Bad Cat Daily — 5.57, 6.7%

*Merged: Bad Cat Daily + Bad Kitty Daily.*

**Pitch.** Everyone gets the same 3D room full of fragile stuff each day. You play your own real cat,
made from one photo, and have 45–90 seconds to wreck as much as you can before the human turns around.
The game cuts a ready-to-post 9:16 clip.

**Core loop.** No-login daily seeded room (kitchen, Christmas tree, wedding cake) in three.js + Rapier.
Swipe-push, jump, tail-swipe, steal the slipper to build combos. Red-light-green-light: the human turns
at random, so you freeze or get caught (reuses Heist guard and detection code). End: Chaos Score,
auto-detected best moment in slow motion, share card and clip. Free: 1 ranked daily run + unlimited
practice on past rooms. Weekly event boss room; a named Pink Paw cat is "criminal of the day".

**UGC.** Your cat is the UGC: /yourcat maps fur colour, pattern and eye colour onto one rigged low-poly
cat. From week 3 the best Trap Rooms player rooms become the daily, credited by name.

**Viral loop.** One-tap 9:16 export with a hook caption, the real cat photo for the first second, a
slow-motion last knock, a CAUGHT / PERFECT CRIME stamp and the URL burned in. Spoiler-free emoji card
(`BAD CAT #212 🏺💥🎄💥🧦 · chaos 8,420 · 0 caught`). Challenge link encodes seed + sender's ghost.
Server-rendered clips can feed faceless cat-POV accounts.

**Monetization.** Chaos Club $3.99–4.99/mo or $29–39/yr (unlimited runs, archive, ghost leagues, no
watermark, extra cats, monthly cosmetic, no ads). Cosmetics $1.99–6.99 visible in every clip.
Self-serve sponsor props from $99/day (upside, not MRR). Published Club share to Pink Paw. Poki/CrazyGames
play-only build as a funnel.

**Visual and stack.** Low-poly toon diorama (Little Kitty Big City / Untitled Goose Game look), fixed
3/4 portrait camera, one-thumb controls. three.js + Rapier WASM + Vite, deterministic fixed-step sim,
under 150 draw calls and ~3 MB first load.

**Fit.** Highest: Heist stealth core, /yourcat onboarding, existing checkouts and shelter-share keeper,
Pink Paw cats as recurring stars, scores via `POST /user/catbassadors/live`.

**Key risks.**
- Clips get views but cat lovers watch and do not play; a shallow 60 s loop tires within a week.
- The "that is MY cat" payoff may not survive: a generic low-poly cat, physics stutter on mid-range
  phones, or failed iOS Safari export makes it an average game clip next to real cat videos.
- Free photoreal AI video (Sora 2, Meow AI) already puts a person's actual cat into a smashed wedding
  cake with one prompt; the genre is held by Catlateral Damage, Roblox BAD CAT and "Knock It Over, Again?".
- $1k MRR needs ~250 Club subscribers, i.e. ~10k weekly actives.
- **Kill tests before building rooms:** render 20 real cats from user photos; run a 2-week gray-box on
  mid-range Android and iOS.

### 2. Catnip Tower — 5.55, 6.0%

**Pitch.** A Suika-style daily physics puzzle: stack squishy 3D loaf-cats (real Pink Paw cats and
community cats) into a wobbly tower. Same seed for everyone, an emoji result card, and every collapse is
worth clipping.

**Core loop.** Daily seed of 20 cats in fixed order, each a named cat with a shape (loaf, long boi,
chonk, donut). Drop and rotate onto a swaying cat tree; two of the same cat merge into a bigger, sleepier
cat. Score = height + merges. A collapse ends the run in slow motion. One ranked attempt, unlimited
practice, weekly friend Tower League.

**UGC.** Players add their cat to the community pool through /yourcat; approved cats appear in others'
seeds with credit ("your cat Mochi was stacked 12,400 times today"). Custom seeds as challenge links.

**Viral loop.** Text card (`CATNIP TOWER #212 🐈🐈‍⬛🐈🟥 · 14 cats · 2 merges · fell at Pumpkin 😾`) plus a
ghost link. Each collapse auto-saves a 9 s slow-motion vertical clip. Owners share "my cat is in today's
tower". Seed in cat/rescue subreddits and VTuber/cozy streamer circles, not dev communities.

**Monetization.** Tower Club $2.99/mo or $24/yr (practice, archive, leagues, custom seeds, no ads).
Ads on free tier and portal builds. "Feature my cat" $4.99. Sponsored daily seed $50–200/day.
Club-funded "Pink Paw treats today" meter.

**Visual and stack.** 2.5D side-on orthographic, pastel toon; squash-and-stretch vertex shader over
Rapier compound colliders; 6 base loaf meshes with photo-sampled palettes; under 3 MB, 60 fps target.

**Fit.** Very good and the cheapest MVP (~2–3 weeks). Works as a fast first ship or a companion daily
to Bad Cat.

**Key risks.**
- Reads as another Suika or cat-stack clone (Cattosu Chonk, Cat Stack); a height score is not personal
  enough to spread like Wordle's grid.
- Design conflict: Suika merging needs repeated pieces, but 20 distinct named cats rarely repeat.
- Cross-device physics determinism for fair daily ranking and ghosts (store ghosts as recorded
  transforms, not re-simulation).
- A $2.99 club at under 1% conversion needs ~50–100k MAU for $1k MRR.
- The "my cat in today's tower" owner loop is the only moat; make it the hero of the launch.

### 3. Cat Runway — 5.44, 5.7%

*Merged: Cat Walk ×2 + Cat Runway.*

**Pitch.** Dress to Impress for your real cat: snap one photo, style your 3D cat for this week's theme in
90 seconds, strut the runway, hit the signature pose, let strangers rate it.

**Core loop.** /yourcat photo → toon 3D cat (the before/after card is the first share). Monday: global
theme ("Met Gala", "Villain Era"). 90 s styling, 6–8 s runway reveal, weekly signature pose. Rate 5 other
cats to unlock your score. Friday: top 10, Best in Show, and a Pink Paw guest model dressed by the
winner. 30 s daily mini-prompt. Friend rooms by link.

**UGC.** Each look (cat identity + outfit + pose + caption) gets a public URL and OG image. "Steal the
look" remix with credit. Peer judging. Phase 2: curated creator accessories with a 50% rev share.

**Viral loop.** Two shares per new user: before/after card, then a 9:16 runway clip ending in
"4.8 ⭐ from 1,203 strangers · rate my cat 1–5". The link opens straight into no-login voting. Weekly
signature pose for owners to film their real cat imitating (the DTI Pose 28 effect).

**Monetization.** Runway Club $3.99–4.99/mo or $29/yr (wardrobe drops, unlimited entries, extra cats,
HD clips, monthly HD portrait). Outfit packs $1.99–4.99, timed drops. Brand theme weeks $500–5k.
"Shelter Couture" / Pink Paw collar with 50–100% shelter share. Gifting.

**Visual and stack.** Cel-shaded chibi cat on a stylised runway; one parametric rigged GLB with blend
shapes, procedural fur from the photo, ~120 rigged accessories; three.js or r3f via `next/dynamic`
(`ssr: false`); MediaRecorder clips, server OG cards. Fallback: PixiJS layered sprites.

**Fit.** Very strong for revenue and audience (cat-owning women 20–40), built on /yourcat, but it shares
the /yourcat launch checklist and reuses little game code.

**Key risks.**
- Vote liquidity: with ~30 actives, "rate 5 to unlock your score" returns empty or fake scores.
- Likeness: if the toon 3D cat does not read as the owner's cat, the identity hook breaks, and free AI
  outfit tools (BeautyPlus, PicLumen, ChatGPT, Gemini) already do "my cat in an outfit" photorealistically.
- A permanent 3D art pipeline (wardrobe growth every week) a 1–2 person team cannot sustain.
- Cheaper first test: a 2D or still-image MVP (photo → styled card → rating link) in 2–3 weeks.

### 4. Steal My Cat: Café Raid — 5.34, 5.0%

*Merged: Steal My Cat + Cat Café Raid.*

**Pitch.** Your cat guards a cozy café-room full of treats and rescued shelter cats. Strangers raid it
through your link while you're away; you wake up to "Biscuit from Kaunas stole Mochi (rare) from your
café" with a revenge button.

**Core loop.** Async base-and-raid (Clash / Steal a Brainrot), no realtime netcode. Decorate a small 3D
room, place 3–5 capped preset defences (sleeping dog, squeaky floor, Roomba, laser). Treats accrue
server-side. Raid via map, friend link or revenge list: a 45–60 s Heist-style stealth run. Global restock
every 5–10 min drops the same rare Pink Paw visitor into everyone's café. Raids stored as seed + inputs;
victims notified by web push or email. Weekly legendary-cat hour.

**UGC.** Every café is a player-authored level with a public no-login raid URL. Collected Pink Paw cats
form a shared cast, each linked to its adoption page. Trading later, seeded by a house trader.

**Viral loop.** Thief gets a 7–10 s replay; victim gets a "you were robbed" card with a revenge link.
Creator dares ("nobody has made it past the Roomba") with the link in a reply. Shared restock moments.

**Monetization.** Café/Cat Burglar Pass 4–6 weeks at $4.99. Café Club $3.99–4.99/mo. No pay-to-win.
Decor visible to every raider. Sponsored furniture. Creator codes at 10–20% for 6 months. Pink Paw
golden collar at 100% shelter share.

**Visual and stack.** 2.5D isometric low-poly toon; grid-snapped JSON room; one shared rig with palette
swaps; three.js + Vite, NestJS/Mongo, restock cron, deterministic replays, Firebase web push.

**Fit.** Strong: gives Catnip Heist the social layer it lacks. 4-week MVP cuts trading; seed bot house
cafés.

**Key risks.**
- Population cold start: bot thefts give no drama worth sharing, so early players meet single-player
  Heist again.
- Platform mismatch: the loop worked inside Roblox's feed and friend graph; the open web has none.
- Heavy server, replay and anti-cheat scope; iOS web push needs a PWA install.
- Brand: real adoptable cats framed as stolen loot; raid results are economy state, not scores.
- Competitors: Roblox CatRaidZ, Pawtopia (browser, Aug 2026), Coin Master.
- Mitigation: ship single-player "rob the Pink Paw house cafés" with replay clips first; add PvP at
  ~1k DAU.

### 5. Cat Derby LIVE — 5.33, 5.0%

*Merged: Cat Derby LIVE + Zoomies Live + Cat Chaos LIVE.*

**Pitch.** A stream-native party game: every viewer who types `!join` drops their own cat into a chaotic
3D race or arena on the host's screen, and gifts drop cucumbers, laser pointers and vacuums.

**Core loop.** Host opens `tokentails.com/derby?host=<handle>` in OBS or a phone screen share. Viewers
join by chat or link as their /yourcat cat (passive viewers auto-played, phone viewers steer). 60–90 s
physics races over a randomised house course or arena modes. Gifts trigger hazards and boosts. Winners
get a persistent crown. Offline: race 20 ghost cats from today's top streams. Weekly Pink Paw Cup.

**UGC.** Viewers' own cats with persistent cat cards; host-built courses from preset modules, shared by
code, rotated into the daily solo course.

**Viral loop.** Host-driven: a game that turns gifts into effects raises host gift income. Every round
auto-clips the funniest 10 s in 9:16. Podium cards ("my cat won on @host's stream") and an overlay link
turn viewers into web players.

**Monetization.** 20% creator rev share for 6 months on joined viewers' spend. Viewer cosmetics
$0.99–4.99. Host Pro $6.99–9.99/mo. Sponsored courses $100–1,000/stream week. Pink Paw charity nights.

**Visual and stack.** Low-poly three.js + Rapier, 100–200 instanced capsule cats; 16:9 broadcast + 9:16
clip crop; sim runs in the host's browser; NestJS WebSocket relay for Twitch (tmi.js/EventSub) and TikTok
LIVE (unofficial tiktok-live-connector); tiny 2D phone controller.

**Fit.** Good: rents other creators' audiences. Best as phase 2 on the Bad Cat / Trap Rooms engine.

**Key risks.**
- Host-side cold start: hundreds of active hosts needed for 10k MAU, recruited by hand.
- The TikTok LIVE connector is unofficial (ToS), breaks often, and TikTok demotes gift-game streams.
- Commodity market: Marbles on Stream, Crowd Control, Livecade, cat/animal gift races already exist.
- Gift money goes to TikTok and the host; chatters rarely upload a photo or pay on Stripe. Expect
  $0–100 MRR at 6 months on the adversarial view.

### 6. Knock It Off: Trap Rooms — 5.09, 4.3%

**Pitch.** Build a 3D room full of breakable stuff and a napping human in 90 seconds, then share the link
and dare anyone to wreck it harder with their own cat in 30 seconds.

**Core loop.** Play: no-login room link, 30 s to knock things off shelves, steal the slipper and trip the
human without waking them. Score = shattered value + combos + stealth bonus. Make: snap-grid editor with
~60 preset props and 3 room shells; publish only after clearing it yourself. Feeds: Daily Wreck, Hot,
"rooms by cats you follow". Every run ends with a 10 s slow-motion replay and the best ghost.

**UGC.** Constrained level authoring (Mario Maker with presets). Public URL + OG preview, "fork this
room" with credit, 24-character filtered title, "your room was wrecked 312 times" digest.

**Viral loop.** The dare is the post ("build a room my cat can't wreck, link in reply"). Challenge link
encodes room id + compressed input replay; result reads "beat Zyg by 1,240".

**Monetization.** Tail Club $4.99/mo or $39/yr (unlimited publishes vs 3 free, prop packs, no
watermark, ghost archive). Cosmetics $1.99–4.99. Sponsor props $49 → $499/week. 10% creator share for
6 months. Pink Paw share of each sponsor slot. Portal build: Daily Wreck only.

**Visual and stack.** Same engine as Bad Cat Daily: toon low-poly rooms, isometric camera, one-thumb
controls, three.js + Rapier + Vite, deterministic physics, MediaRecorder with ffmpeg fallback, rooms and
replays in NestJS + Mongo + object storage.

**Fit.** Strong as Bad Cat Daily's phase 2 / creator mode.

**Key risks.**
- Two-sided cold start: dares need creators, creators come only from dares; creators are 1–3% of
  players.
- The paid tier charges for the rarest behaviour (publishing).
- Build is 8–12 weeks, not 3–4 (deterministic ghosts, touch 3D editor, iOS clip export).
- Mitigation: ship play-only Daily Wreck with house-built rooms and clip export first.

### 7. Infinite Litter — 4.82, 4.0%

**Pitch.** Combine anything with a cat to discover a new cat (Toast + Cat = Loaf Cat, Loaf Cat + Fire =
Croissant Dragon); your cat is credited forever as the world's first discoverer.

**Core loop.** Start with Cat, Water, Fire, Yarn; drag two together. Cat results become 2.5D parametric
cats (body, pattern, ears, tail, prop, aura chosen by an LLM from a strict parts schema). Results cached
globally; first maker credited. Daily: "today's mystery cat" (a transformed Pink Paw cat), solve in
fewest combos. Catdex collection.

**UGC.** Recombination UGC (Infinite Craft): players build the graph and name species once. Public
species pages. Skins apply to your /yourcat cat across Token Tails games.

**Viral loop.** First Discovery card; spoiler-free daily path (`🐱+🔥 → 🥐 → ✅ in 4`); species pages as
challenge targets. Works better on X and screenshots than TikTok.

**Monetization.** Litter Club $2.99/mo (archive, hints, HD/3D-printable poster, renames, no ads).
Sponsor elements in the graph, sponsored daily cat, rewarded hint ads on portals. Daily cat links to
adopt or sponsor.

**Visual and stack.** 2.5D cozy tabletop with chip UI; results as 3D toon cats from one parametric rig
(never AI illustrations); one Claude Haiku / Gemini Flash JSON call per new pair, cached in Mongo.

**Fit.** Cheap MVP (2–3 weeks), lowest cost per player once the cache warms; weak on clips.

**Key risks.**
- Cold-start paradox: with ~0 players every combo is a "first", so the brag is worthless.
- An Infinite Craft clone without Neal Agarwal's launch audience; the format's proven model is free.
- One parametric rig caps the punchline ("Croissant Dragon" = cat + prop + aura).
- Name moderation, bot discovery farms, daily puzzle curation as ongoing ops.

### 8. Cat City: One Million Cats — 4.59, 3.7%

*Merged: Cat City + Cat Street.*

**Pitch.** One persistent shared 3D town where every cat a player makes lives forever. A global clock
brings shelter-cat visits and fish-truck restocks to everyone at once, and a live counter ties the town's
growth to real Pink Paw goals.

**Core loop.** Make your cat, pick a plot on an endless isometric map, give it a home. Daily: feed and
play (Tamagotchi-light, no punishment), visit neighbours, collect a treat. Fish Truck restocks every few
minutes; a named Pink Paw cat wanders through at set times and feeding it fills a community treat meter
that triggers a real donation. Sponsor-funded milestone targets ("at 10,000 cats, Pickles gets a heated
bed"). Light presence first; stealing only once there is a population.

**UGC.** Every cat and home is player-made and permanent; public `/cat/<name>` pages; streets named for
shelter cats; later creator "street takeovers".

**Viral loop.** Zoom-out shot from your cat to the whole town ("my cat is one of 48,213 now"). "Be my
neighbour" link reserves the adjacent plot. Milestone and visit moments. Hub for cats from the clip games.

**Monetization.** Sponsored named world objects via Stripe ladder (spike catcher). Resident+
$2.99–4.99/mo. Home cosmetics, premium plots. Sponsors fund the shelter meter. Creator codes 15% for 6
months.

**Visual and stack.** Chunked isometric world, InstancedMesh with LOD to sprites, CDN-cached chunk
snapshots from NestJS/Mongo, Redis global clock, optional sharded WebSocket presence.

**Fit.** Strong brand fit as a hub; weak as an acquisition tool. Launch only after a clip game proves
traction.

**Key risks.**
- Empty-world cold start: the share hooks need density that does not exist on day one.
- Hosting and moderation cost peak with a spike (One Million Checkboxes); sponsors decay to $0.
- "Lives forever" conflicts with photo moderation and GDPR erasure.
- At most a thin `/cat/<name>` gallery map behind a proven clip game.

## Cross-cutting risks

- **K < 1 everywhere.** All loops amplify seeded reach; none compounds by itself. Plan for many clip
  iterations on faceless accounts and niche seeding (r/cats, rescue communities), not one launch.
- **Cold start.** Café Raid, Cat Runway, Trap Rooms, Cat Derby and Cat City all need a population before
  their core loop works. Ship single-player or house-seeded slices first.
- **The "my real cat" hook.** Most concepts lean on /yourcat. If a recoloured rig does not read as the
  owner's cat, the hook loses to free photoreal AI pet tools. /yourcat also has an open launch checklist
  (App Check, billed Gemini key, controller and privacy text, ToS).
- **Brand tone.** "Bad cat", "wreck the house", "steal shelter cats" and gift hazards can clash with a
  shelter adoption message; keep the framing affectionate ("coax", "visit").
- **Shared technical traps.** Cross-device deterministic Rapier physics for ghosts, iOS Safari
  MediaRecorder clip export (needs a server fallback), mobile WebGL performance with many bodies.
- **Score path.** Any concept must save scores only through `POST /user/catbassadors/live`.
- **Team time.** The funding calendar (Tameion Oct 17, Anitya Oct 21, Arbitrum Dubai Nov 16 – Dec 6)
  competes with any 4–12 week build.
