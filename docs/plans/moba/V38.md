# Sim v38: the human's allies lane like the enemy bots

Founder ask (2026-11-19): "also there's a problem with bots. currently they're following me always, they should lane in
a similar way like enemies bots do as well. as it's difficult to understand and lane in this current way."

**Why a new sim version.** It changes what every Rookie and Easy match computes (Skirmish on the five 3v3 maps, Blitz,
Classic 5v5), so a stored v37 Easy replay no longer verifies. v37 is frozen like every earlier bump. Normal, Hard and
PvP matches are computed exactly as on v37 (§5).

## 1. Why the allies followed the human (v37)

`DifficultyRules.carryAllies` was 2 on Rookie **and Easy** (`src/data/bots.ts`). Easy is what every player gets in
matches 2 and 3 and the default pick after that, so most solo matches ran it. A "carry" ally (`src/sim/bots/brain.ts`
`think`, sim v4/v5):

- played the human's lane (`laneFor` returned `humanLane`, wherever the human stood), not its own slot lane;
- walked back to the human whenever it was more than 7 m away (`BOT_AI.followRange`) and never stood more than 4 m ahead
  of it (`leadMax`);
- skipped the map objective, the rat camp, Grizzlechop, the jungle route and the yarn ball, and left Frenzy Catnip to
  the human.

The leash (`humanEngaged`) let go only when the human stood still for 8 s or left the lanes, so a human who kept
playing was shadowed by both allies all match. The enemy bots never did this: they lane by slot role (Sunny, Puddle,
the roamer), the lane swap and grouping. Measured on v37 (`tools/ally-spread.mjs`, 400 matches, seeds 50,001+, slot 0
a Normal-level player-bot, share of seconds 0:30–5:00 within 10 m of its team's lead hero):

| Easy, v37 | Allies near the human | Enemy bots near their lead (slot 3) |
|---|---|---|
| 3v3 pool rotation | **73.3%** (in the human's lane 99.7%) | 35.7% (same lane 60.3%) |
| Purrhaven Crossing | **76.2%** | 42.2% |
| Blitz | **63.8%** | 24.6% |
| Classic 5v5 (Hearthwood) | **64.2%** (4 allies) | 6.2% |

On Normal and Hard `carryAllies` was already 0 and allies and enemies matched (24.0% / 24.0%).

## 2. What v38 does

- `carryAllies`: Easy 2 → **0**; Rookie 2 → **1** (the roamer only). Normal, Hard and PvP stay 0.
- New rule `DifficultyRules.helpAllies` (Rookie 2, Easy 2, Normal, Hard and PvP 0): an ally that does not follow still
  **peels** (an enemy hero hitting the human is its first target, from a little further away) and **leaves the human a
  nearly dead foe** (`leaveToHuman`), but only in a fight it already reaches from its own lane. It never walks to the
  human for it. `tests/unit/pvp-rules.test.ts` classifies it as one-sided (0 in PvP).
- Everything else an Easy ally does is the enemy bots' code path: its slot lane, the roamer's lane choice (the lane with
  fewer own towers, then the more hurt laner), the rat camp, Pudding, the pool maps' objectives, base defence, grouping
  at the Easy `groupAt`, the Hearthwood roles and jungle route. Its skill (`ALLIES.easy`, the Normal table with Easy's
  `groupAt`) is unchanged.
- **The human's lane is chosen by where the human goes.** That is the existing lane swap (`bots/orders.ts`
  `stepLaneSwap`, `stepRoleSwap` on Hearthwood): 5 s in another lane (from 0:40) and the ally whose lane it is takes
  the human's old lane. A human who walks off does not pull anyone along; a bot changes lanes only as an enemy bot does
  (grouping, the roamer's choice, base defence, objectives).
- **Rookie (match 1) keeps light tutorial help**, as POC.md §Sim v4 intends ("Rookie is match 1 only: the human's two
  allies carry"): one ally, the roamer, still stays with the human; the Puddle laner now plays Puddle. The scripted first
  fight, the enemies coming to the human's lane (`enemiesToHuman`) and the human's buffs are unchanged.

## 4. Win rates and the human's own match

Slot 0 is a player-bot: `naive` (the scripted first-timer of the fun gates, it chases any hero and ignores towers),
`novice` (follows the first-match tips) or `normal` (the bot brain from outside, a competent player).

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rookie, naive (fun1) | Crossing, 200 | 100.0% | **100.0%** | 2 / 12 → 3 / 8 |
| Rookie, naive | pool, 500 (60,001+) | 98.4% | **100.0%** | 2 / 13 → 2 / 9 |
| Rookie, normal | pool, 500 | 100.0% | **100.0%** | 1 / 16 → 1 / 14 |

Read honestly:

- Rookie's fun1 gate: v37 failed four checks (deaths p90 5, length p95 7:25, core kill before late Sunset 84.5%,
  90%-to-dead 6.1 s); v38 fails three (deaths p90 6, length median 4:58 against 5:00–6:00, 90%-to-dead 6.1 s): the
  matches got 18 s shorter.
- **First-timers win as often as before** (within ±2 points on every Rookie and Easy gate with a scripted newcomer),
  but **their own match is harder**: alone in a lane, the naive player dies about twice as often on Easy (median 5 → 9)
  and takes half the takedowns. The fun2 gate's "deaths ≤ 4" check already failed on v37 (5); "takedowns ≥ 4" now fails
  too (3). The novice (who follows the tips) goes from 2 to 3–4 deaths.
- **A competent player now wins Easy about 90% of matches** (68% on v37). On v37 two of three heroes stood in one lane
  and the other lane fell; now the human's team covers the map with Normal-skill allies against Easy bots. Normal is
  unchanged (the competent player-bot wins 50% there), so Normal is the difficulty for a fair match.
- Tried and not shipped (200 + 400 + 400 seeds each): Easy allies at Easy skill (`ALLIES.easy = BOTS.easy`) bring the
  competent player back to 68.3% but the novice to 77.5% (fails ≥ 85%) and the naive to 63.5% (fails 70–80%);
  blended skills (reaction 13–16 ticks, fewer abilities, no wave clear or dives) and human-only help (taken × 0.85–0.9,
  ×2–3 regen, spared by enemy bots) moved nothing outside noise (competent 85–89%, naive deaths 8–9).
- Comebacks on Easy rose: the team behind on takedowns at 3:30 now wins 60–75% (v37 25–64%), because the human's team
  is the one that trails on kills and wins on the map.

## 5. Normal, Hard, PvP and the frozen numbers

Nothing on those paths changed: `carryAllies` and `helpAllies` are 0 there, and the new `help` flag is false. Proof:

- `tests/unit/sim-v38.test.ts` re-sims every v37 Normal, Hard and PvP record (Crossing late and forfeit, Blitz, the four
  pool maps, the Hearthwood party and PvP records, the 3v3 PvP record) relabelled 38: same final hash and ticks. The
  backend spec does the same with the vendored v37 records. A v37 Easy record relabelled 38 is refused.
- 200 pool-rotation Normal matches (seeds 110,001+) and 200 Blitz matches (seeds 30,001+) give the same final hash on
  v37 and v38, 400 of 400.

So the balance gates are v37's, match for match. Held-out runs on v38 for this report:

The held-out 8,000-match 3v3 pool run (seeds 110,001+), the 8,000-match Blitz run (seeds 30,001+) and the 5v5
symmetry run (1,000 line-ups played twice, seeds 160,001+) are in §5a.

### The two Ridge safety ends of the v37 report

Both reproduce on v38 (Normal is unchanged): seeds 101,939 and 102,544 of the `--gate balance --map pool` window
100,001+. Neither is a stuck or a broken bot. Both are even matches that become a base race: 101,939 is 9–10 takedowns
at 11:00 with the Purrhaven core at 10% and Woof's at 70% (Woof keeps one Sunny tower); 102,544 is 16–19 with every tower
down and both cores at 20–30% from 9:30 on, each push wiped at the defended core and answered the other way. The safety
end gives the win to the side ahead on the tie-break (Woof both times). 2 in 8,000 (0.03%). Not changed in v38: any rule
that ends these would change Normal and needs its own balance run. If wanted later: on the pool maps, raise
`POOL_LATE_TAKEDOWN_CORE_BP` past 10:00 so a won fight at a 20% core ends it.

## 6. Freeze, backend and release

- `SIM_VERSION` 38. v37 is frozen at aaa8033d (its sim commit on main, byte for byte the live v37 dir) with every
  fixture. The backend verifies 24 to 38 (fifteen sims; v20–v23 retired in v37). Local `nest build` peak RSS:
  2.26 and 2.29 GB (two runs; v24–v37 was 2.17–2.19 GB, and DigitalOcean's 2 GB build failed from
  2.62 GB), so no sim is retired.
- Release order (FORCE-UPDATE.md, LIVE-MATCH-DEPLOYS.md): the backend first (it accepts 37 and 38), then the game build
  and the OTA, whose deploy gate checks that the backend serves the v38 bundle. A v37 client keeps saving v37 matches.
- New tools: `tools/ally-spread.mjs` (the §1/§3 measure) and `tools/ridge-safety.mjs` (finds a map's safety ends in a
  balance window, with the gate's exact seeding).
