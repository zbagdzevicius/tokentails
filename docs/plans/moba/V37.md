# Sim v37: bots commit to a walk goal, the Blitz refit

Two gaps the v34 to v36 reports left open (V34-MAPS.md §2, V35-BACKLOG.md §2, V36-BALANCE.md §5):

- **Stuck bots on the 3v3 maps.** A bot's think (every 6 ticks) alternated between two goals far apart (the lane
  front and its back-off point, Pudding and the lane, a gem and a back-off). Each new Move restarted its walk, so it
  stood still: per 200 matches, Thicket 46, Park 28, Quarry 27, Ridge 19 and Crossing 16 matches had a stuck bot.
- **Blitz** has its own per-hero rows (`MUTATOR_RULES.blitz.hero`), which no pass since v33 refit: 15 heroes outside
  46–54% on v36, cats 53.1%, and a team down 10+ takedowns won 5.1% (gate < 5%).

**Why a new sim version.** Both change what every 3v3 and Blitz match computes, so a stored v36 replay would no longer
verify. v36 is frozen like every earlier bump. Hearthwood (Classic 5v5) plays exactly as on v36 (§5).

## 1. Method

- Stuck bots: `node tools/maps-sanity.mjs --map <id> --seeds 200 --from 1001` (a bot trying to move with less than
  0.3 m of progress a second for 5 s), before and after on the same seeds.
- 3v3 gates: `npm run match -- --gate balance --map pool --seeds 8000 --from 100001`, the seeds of the v36 report.
- Blitz: `npm run match -- --gate blitz --seeds 8000 --stats`. Rows fitted on seeds 1–8,000 (a ridge logistic fit over
  the six-hero line-ups, about 0.032 logit per 1% of a row, damped 0.7 then 0.5, half-percent steps; dealt and taken
  move the same amount the other way), verified once on unseen seeds 20,001–28,000.
- Kills and deaths: `node tools/deaths-probe.mjs --map pool --seeds 1000 --from 80001`, the v36 report's seeds.

## 2. Commitment (`src/sim/bots/brain.ts` `holdWalk3`, `BOT_AI.walkHold` / `walkFar`)

One rule for the 3v3 maps (Crossing and the pool maps), in the bots' think:

- A bot's current walk goal is its Move point, or the unit it attacks while that unit is alive and out of attack range.
- A new goal more than `walkFar` (4 m) from it, less than `walkHold` (60 ticks, 2 s) after the last far switch,
  waits: the bot keeps walking where it was going. Any far switch that goes through restarts the clock.
- Never held: a retreat (`BOT_MODE.Retreat`), a back-off from untanked tower fire, an attack on a unit already in
  attack range (no walk), and any goal within 4 m of the current one (lane-front adjustments stay smooth).
- The clock is new world state, `World.botWalkAt` (hashed; one slot per hero, empty on Hearthwood). An outside
  player-bot keeps its own (`BotMem.walkAt`).

Hearthwood keeps its own hold (`JUNGLE_BOT.goalHold`, `mg`), unchanged.

Lane-front spreading was not needed: with the hold, the lane-front jam by the enemy outer tower went from the most
common case to a handful (the remaining samples are small dithers within 4 m, and a Park bot pushing a ball resting
on a crate).

## 3. Blitz rout (`MutatorRules.routTakedowns`, `src/sim/match.ts` `timeUpWinner`)

All 27 Blitz matches a team down 10+ takedowns won (of 356, 8.1%, v37 bots with the v36 rows) ended at the 3:15 time
up with that team one or two towers ahead. Rows cannot fix that. New tie-breaker order at the time up: **a team 8 or
more takedowns ahead wins (a rout)**, then towers, core HP share, takedowns and structure damage as before. Blitz only
(`routTakedowns: 8`); every other mode keeps 0 (Cannon Chaos keeps the old order).

## 6. Freeze, backend build memory and release

- `SIM_VERSION` 37. v36 is frozen at df342432 (its sim commit on main; the v36 dir is byte for byte the live one) with
  every fixture.
- Backend build memory (local `nest build` peak RSS, macOS `/usr/bin/time -l`, two runs each): v20–v36 (live before)
  2.54–2.55 GB; **v20–v37 2.63 GB**, the level of v10–v34 that ran out of memory on DigitalOcean's 2 GB build. So v37
  **retires v20, v21, v22 and v23**: **v24–v37 2.17–2.19 GB**. Why those: v20 stopped being live on 2026-11-11 and
  v21 to v23 on 2026-11-12, before the first client that obeys the force-update policy (sim v27, 2026-11-14);
  the OTA channel's `minSim` is 35 (compat 2, iOS and Android), the web always loads the live build. A device-queued
  match of v20 to v23 is refused with `PAWLEGENDS_SIM_VERSION` and purged by the host, as v10 to v19 were. The backend
  verifies 24 to 37; its effective `minSim` floor becomes 24.
- Release order (FORCE-UPDATE.md, LIVE-MATCH-DEPLOYS.md): the backend first (it accepts 36 and 37), then the game
  build and the OTA, which raise `minSim` to 37. The party relay refuses mixed builds as before.
