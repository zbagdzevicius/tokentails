## 1. Tower balance: root causes, not looser gates

### 1.A Executions (`progression/xp.ts` `executed`, `combat/damage.ts`)

**Root cause of the comeback failures.** Every hero death counted as a takedown for the other team, also a death to a
tower, a minion, a camp or the map with no enemy hero involved. v26 made towers hit much harder (towers now land 17% to
20% of killing blows), so a team that was ahead and dived towers fed the trailing team "takedowns": each one added to
its kill score, chipped the diver's own towers (the chip goes to the victim team's weakest tower), and after Sunset hit
the diver's core. The `map5v5-probe` down-10 matches show it: the leader was 10 to 18 takedowns ahead around 7:00 to
9:00 and the final score was nearly level, with the leader's core lower at 13:00. 3v3 "down 10+ wins" went from 1.7%
(v25) to 6.6% (v26) for the same reason.

**Rule (Wild Rift and LoL "executed").** A hero that dies with no enemy hero credited (none landed the last hit or hit
it within `XP.assistWindow`, 10 s; on a `nearAssists` difficulty none alive within `XP.shareRange`) was executed. An
execution adds nothing to the other team's kill score and deals no takedown chip, no Sunset or Sudden Death takedown
core damage. Its XP, Fish, death streak and respawn are unchanged. A tower kill with a hero's help is still a takedown
for that hero.

### 1.B Hearthwood chip floor (`HEARTHWOOD_RULES.chipFloorBp` 5500)

Five heroes a side score about 5/3 the takedowns of the 3v3 match the chip was tuned for, so 32% to 38% of 5v5 first
towers fell mostly to chips (T7, gate ≤ 20%). Executions alone did not move it (44% on v27 probe run A): the chips came
from real takedowns. On Hearthwood a chip now never takes a tower below 55% of its max HP (Crossing keeps 15%), so chips
soften a 5v5 tower and heroes and minions take it. A ×0.6 chip scale (run B) still left 31%; dropped.

### 1.C Fortified hero share per difficulty (`DifficultyRules.fortifyHeroBp`, was `TOWERS.fortifyBp`)

On Hearthwood Easy the human's team (four carry allies) took an enemy outer tower at a 3:34 median, inside the 5:30
Fortified window, heroes doing 80% of the damage (T2, target 4:30 to 6:00). Easy's heroes now deal 20% to a Fortified
tower; Rookie, Normal, Hard and PvP keep 55%. On the Crossing it moves Easy's first tower from 3:30 to about 4:05,
inside its 3:30 to 4:30 band (T4; the normal player was 3:27, just under, in v26).

### 1.D Hearthwood Fortified 5:00 and outer towers 2,300 HP

With chips capped, a 5v5 first tower stood longer (Normal 6:59, PvP 7:33, Hard 7:42 in run D). Hearthwood's Fortified
window goes back to 5:00 (`TOWERS.fortifyUntil[1]` 9900 → 9000, the v25 value) and its outer towers to 2,300 base HP
(3,000; 3,105 on Normal and Hard after `structureHpBp`). Inner, gate and Hearth Guard towers are unchanged.

Tried and dropped: no catch-up XP on Hearthwood (one window 5.3% down-10 wins, the next 13.7% and 23%, and cats fell to
44.7% in `hearth-gates`), the Great Pudding at 7:00 (no effect on Hard), a Hard chip of 20% and a 4:30 window (Hard
7:38). Hard's first tower is not durability-bound: Hard towers are first hit at ~2:50 and fall after ~4:40 of trading
poke; the cluster at 8:00 to 9:00 follows the Great Pudding.

## 2. Practice ground (`mutator: 'practice'`, `data/practice.ts`, `sim/units/practice.ts`)

Mode Select → **Practice** → "Solo with training dummies" → any champion → LOCK IN. The Crossing with no lane waves and
no Pudding; the cores never open; the bots never think, so the allies wait at the fountain. The enemy team's three
slots are training dummies: one stands on Sunny Lane, one paces Puddle Lane, one waits under the enemy's Sunny outer
tower (hitting it draws that tower's fire). Dummies never strike or cast and come back on their spot 2 s after a
takedown; the player's respawn is 2 s and Fish comes ×3 to try Toys.

Controls: `Op.Practice {kind}` (2 bytes in the stream): 0 Reset (dummies back on their spots at full HP, the player's
HP and cooldowns full, standing towers repaired), 1 Level +1, 2 Cooldowns. They sit in the top plate where the team
score is (`.pl-practice`), so the phone HUD keeps its footprint. Pause → Leave goes straight Home: no results, no
`/live`, no XP, no quests, no match count. The match ends at the map's 11:00 safety end.

## 3. Cannon Chaos (`mutator: 'cannon'`)

3v3 on the Crossing vs bots, about 5 minutes: the Kibble Cannon at 0:15 and 20 s after every shot or fizzle (the
Underdog Cannon favours a team 3 takedowns behind), start at level 3 with XP ×1.5, cooldowns ×0.7, towers ×0.7 and
cores ×0.8 HP, Fortified until 1:00, respawns 5 s rising to 7 s, Sunset 3:00, Sudden Death 4:15, time up at 5:15
(decided like Blitz). Saved through `/live` like Blitz (per-minute Season XP, no ladder, no fastest win, no dare).

Sanity (40 matches, Normal player-bot vs Normal bots): median 4:32, p95 4:39, every match a core kill, sides 21 / 19,
4.35 takedowns a minute (Skirmish 3.40).

## 4. Weekly Twist (`toybox`, `rapid`, `bounty`; backend `liveops.defaults.ts`)

A rotating rule set on Skirmish vs bots, one a week (Monday 00:00 UTC), in the sim's `WEEKLY_TWISTS` order:

| Twist | Rule |
|---|---|
| Toy Box | Fish ×2 (Toys come twice as fast); Frenzy Catnip and Fish Snacks twice as often |
| Rapid Paws | every ability and Paw Trick cooldown ×0.6 |
| Bounty Hunt | a takedown streak's bounty XP and the shutdown Fish ×3 |

`GET /pawlegends/config` answers `twist: {id, name, rules, endsAt}` and, by default, `featuredMode: {id: 'twist'}`:
the twist is the week's featured mode. The game registers a "Weekly Twist" tile (an event with its countdown) on the
one play path. Config doc: `twist: {id, endsAt}` picks one, `twist: null` turns it off (the old mode rotation comes
back), a doc `featuredMode` still wins. Offline the game plays the same baked rotation. Twist matches save like Blitz.

Sanity (40 matches each, Normal vs Normal): Skirmish 7:40 / 3.40 takedowns a minute; Toy Box 8:00 / 3.56; Rapid Paws
7:48 / 3.59; Bounty Hunt 7:59 / 3.28; every match a core kill.
