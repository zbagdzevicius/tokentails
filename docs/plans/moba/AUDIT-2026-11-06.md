# Paw Legends audit report (sim v12, 2026-11-06)

Scope: UX, touch and desktop controls, match gameplay and balance, graphics and performance. Tested at 667x375 and 844x390 (touch) and 1440x900, with about 10 browser matches and more than 2,500 headless seeds. All evidence is under `scratchpad/audit/` (`ux/`, `controls/`, `gameplay/`, `gfx/`). No finding was refuted. Several were downgraded after verification, and those changes are noted.

## Verdict

1. On touch, aiming W up or E up-left cancels the cast (8 of 8 drags). This is the most damaging bug for a phone player.
2. The dog side is 2 melee heroes for 3 slots, so every dog team has a twin. The picker has no CATS/DOGS switch. This is the user's request.
3. Phone layouts overflow: the lobby, the pick-screen kit, the results rewards and the Treat Track/Settings exits all sit below the fold.
4. The clock ends matches, not the push. 79.5% end after Sunset, and only Pudding decides fights, so matches feel scripted.
5. In match 1, an idle player wins 50.5% and is told "with your help". The first lesson is "waiting wins".
6. Enemy dog buildings are navy blue while blue now means ally, and fights on phones bury heroes under plates and shout-words.

Performance is fine: 58-60 fps on Med/High at 6x CPU throttle, input latency 1-3 frames.

## 1. Hero pick and the dog side (user request)

- **No CATS/DOGS switch (UX-02, GFX-09, GP-10). Confirmed, high.** One 12-card grid: 10 cats, then Sir Brutus and Cacklejaw as tiles 11-12. Phone tiles show only the name, so you cannot see a hero's side before you tap it. The detail panel does show it ("Cacklejaw Dog Assassin"). At 667 the Clem tile clips at the left edge. Evidence: `ux/667x375-f5-pick-dog.png`, `ux/844x390-pick.png`, `gfx/667x375-02-pick.png`, `gameplay/pick-667x375.png`.
  Fix: a segmented switch, CATS (paw, rose) | DOGS (bone, mustard). Default to the last side. Add tile badges and flex-wrap. Until the new dogs ship, fill empty DOGS slots with "coming soon" silhouettes and show "vs Cats" on the pick screen.
- **Dog teams always repeat a hero (GP-01, GFX-02). Known as GAPS N4; the new numbers make it the top item.** `src/sim/index.ts:108-117` fillPicks falls back to kin once fresh dogs run out. Dog teams had a duplicate in 400/400 seeds, with no ranged hero, mage or support. Brutus and Cacklejaw take about 1,200 slots each in 800 seeds, against about 230 per cat. Evidence: `gameplay/dog-2.png` (both allies the same Cacklejaw), `gfx/sheet-clem.png`, `ux/1440x900-f6-tab.png`.
  Correction to the GFX stopgap: Cacklejaw's Track coats are already wired (GAPS A0 done, `specs.ts:380`). What is missing is giving the duplicate bot a different coat.
  Dog concepts that read from the top-down camera: Corgi tank (long and low), Chihuahua assassin (huge ears), Greyhound marksman (thin, tall), Husky or sneezing Pug mage (AoE sneeze), Golden Retriever support who "fetches" allies back. For the viral joke dog: a hot-dog-costume Dachshund, or a "Doge" with a shades emote. Use the beast.ts/chibi kit and the HERO_BUILD ranges. Run the per-species balance gate after each addition.
- **Side-blind copy (UX-07, GP-10). Confirmed, medium.** `screens.ts:627` hard-codes "Switch cat". `tryHeroFor` (`app.ts:117-122`) ignores side, so a dog main is pushed to a cat. Evidence: `ux/667x375-f5-res.png`, `gameplay/dog-end.png`. Also reword "Easy bots. More bots after 3 matches." to "Normal and Hard bots unlock after 3 matches."
- **Kit text clipped on phones (UX-06). Confirmed, medium.** At 667 the Q, E and R rows are cut and the R row sits below the viewport. Fix: one line per ability below 420 px of height, full text on tap, ult first.

LoL/MLBB comparison: MLBB and Wild Rift filter the grid with one tap on role tabs, never allow duplicates on a team, and give every faction every role.

## 2. Touch and desktop controls

Latency is good: 18-52 ms from touch to movement, 82 ms to reverse direction. The problems are layout and aim design. Evidence: `controls/` (`cancel.out`, `abil.out`, `layout.out`, `lane.out`, `desk.out`, screenshots `abil-844-E-drag.png`, `abil-667-pounce-right.png`, `abil-667-R-locked-tap.png`).

- **CF-01 Cancel trap. Confirmed, critical.** The X sits at `ui.css:436-438` with 12 px of hit slack, 86-91 px from E and W. A full-range aim needs a 90 px drag. All 8 up and up-left drags cancelled. Commit 78d913fa moved the chip there on purpose. Wild Rift and MLBB put cancel at the top of the screen, out of every drag's reach.
- **CF-04 Button overlap. Confirmed, high.** E and trick overlap by 5 px. W-R, Q-nap, W-E, ATK-Q and Q-W have gaps of 13-18 px. The layout is fixed CSS px, so 667 gets the same layout as 844.
- **CF-03 Pounce range. Confirmed, high.** A tap blinks 2.4 of 4 m (`targeting.ts:210` ahead(0.6)). A drag toward the right edge reaches 1.56 m. A tap ignores the stick for point aims. In MLBB and Wild Rift, Flash goes full distance on a tap.
- **CF-06 No aim ring at the thumb. Confirmed, medium.** The 90 px full-range and 24 px smart zones are invisible.
- **CF-07 Silent fail on unit-target ults (Brutus R, Raspberry R). Confirmed, medium.** `orders.ts:348` returns false and touch ignores it. Desktop plays the deny sound.
- **CF-08 Smart cast walks under an enemy tower. Confirmed by code, medium.** smartAim and walkIn never check guardedByTower, while ATK avoids it on purpose.
- **CF-10 / UX-11 Enemy lock portraits 28-30 px. Confirmed, medium.** They are below the 44 px minimum. The edge indicators overlapping the cluster is intermittent and partial (UX-11 is plausible only).
- **CF-05 Phone camera framing. Downgraded to medium.** At 667 the button cluster covers ground past 5.8 m ahead, and only 4.8 m shows below the hero. At 844 the cluster starts at 8.7 m, which clears every range. Add a look-ahead toward the aim and lower the shortBelow lift.
- **CF-02 Last hits. Downgraded to medium.** Hero-first ATK is the documented A5 design. The new defect is that ATK (`targeting.ts:121`, hero first) and the idle auto-attack (`assist.ts:122`, minion first) disagree. Make them agree.
- Low: CF-09 the locked R on touch gives no "Lv 4" hint. CF-11 there is no button size or layout setting (`touchLayout.js` already has the solver). CF-12 a denied cast on desktop gives sound only, no slot flash. CF-13 the edge pan snaps back after 3 s (by design; an unlocked-camera option). CF-14 the joystick base is not clamped off the edges.

## 3. UX and screens

Works well: one tap into a guided first match, "Run it back", the ping wheel, Tab scores, Forfeit with a confirm, the death recap, quests, the Treat Track, grade and MVP.

- **UX-01 Lobby overflow for returning players. Confirmed, high.** At 844x390 the column spans top -12 to bottom 402, and at 667x375 it spans -37 to 412, with no scroll. Evidence: `ux/844-ret-title.png`, `ux/667x375-f5-title.png`, `gfx/p-844-lobby.png` (the skin teaser sits on the pedestal, GFX-13).
- **UX-03 Free one-tap Leave. Confirmed, medium-high.** `screens.ts:705`, `matchSession.ts:1105-1108`, `app.ts:822-826`. Nothing is recorded, so Forfeit is bypassed and the forfeit-rate measure is skewed. Evidence: `ux/667-leave1.png`. LoL, Wild Rift and Brawl Stars all count a quit as a loss.
- **UX-04 Results bury rewards. Confirmed, medium-high.** On phones scrollHeight is 995 against clientHeight 374, and the Treat XP, chest, quests and share are off screen. On desktop only "Copy dare link" is below the fold. Evidence: `ux/844x390-res-0.png`, `ux/667x375-f5-res.png`, `ux/1440x900-f4-res4.png`. Wild Rift and MLBB use a separate rewards page with a Continue tap.
- **UX-05 Pause > Scores renders under the pause card. Confirmed, medium.** `.pl-scoreboard` has z-index 8 and `.pl-menu` 40 (`ui.css:319`, `:178`). It is the only scoreboard path on touch. Evidence: `ux/667-scores.png`.
- **UX-08 No Home on results, and Back keeps the "Try" hero. Confirmed, medium.** `app.ts:1027-1032`, `:713`. Evidence: `ux/1440x900-f4-title-ret.png`.
- **UX-09 No Settings in the pause menu. Confirmed, medium.** Every rival opens full settings in a match.
- **UX-10 Treat Track and Settings exits below the fold at 667. Confirmed, medium.** No corner X. Esc does not close the Treat Track (`collection.ts:85-101`), though Settings does handle Esc. Evidence: `ux/667x375-f5-treat.png`.
- Low: UX-12 the results "Hero" column shows bot pet names with no hero (add portraits; avoid the bot name "Tank"). UX-13 no boot splash, a blank purple screen until JS runs (the time was measured at load 36, so its real length is unknown).

## 4. Match gameplay and balance

Healthy: dead time is a median 7.6% of the match, kills matter on Normal (down 10+ wins 1.5-3%), Paw Tricks sit at 47.8-52.2%, the cat species wins 52.1%, there are no safety ends, and the pace is 3.47 takedowns a minute. Evidence: `gameplay/` (`balance400*.json`, `novice-normal.json`, `nov-*.txt`, `naive-easy.json`, `idle-rookie.json`, `track-*.json`).

- **GP-05 Idle player wins match 1. Confirmed, high.** It wins 50.5% (the gate is 50% or less). `screens.ts:518-522` says "jump into a fight" only at 0 hero damage, and only 4 of 101 idle wins had 0 damage. Evidence: `gameplay/844x390-end.png` ("with your help" at 0/0/1). In LoL's tutorial, the player is scripted as the cause of the win.
- **GP-03 The clock ends matches. Confirmed, high.** 82/400 end before Sunset, 285 between Sunset and Sudden Death, and 33 after. The p25-p75 spread is 55 s. The winner took only 2 towers in 38.5% of matches. (Correction: Rookie Sudden Death is 6:30, not 6:15.) LoL and Wild Rift end on structures, and a stomp ends early.
- **GP-02 Novices on Normal reach 40% only on dogs. Confirmed, high.** Brutus wins 47.0% and Cacklejaw 40.5%, against Raspberry 32.0%, Sage 30.0% and Duchess 29.5%. The overall 32% is known in GAPS; the per-hero split is new. Only 5 heroes were tested. Fix: tune Normal for the human's ranged roles, add "Beginner friendly" tags, and a step between Easy (79%) and Normal.
- **GP-04 Only Pudding decides. Plausible, high.** More Pudding calms wins 82.1%, and a camp lead wins 37.2%. The camp result is mostly a side effect of `campBehindBp 15000` (`bots.ts`, `xp.ts:205`), which sends trailing bots to farm camps. Make camps a contested objective and move the comeback bonus elsewhere. LoL and Wild Rift have 5-7 objective fights a game.
- **GP-06 Lulls. Plausible, medium.** The longest takedown gap has a median of 55 s and p90 of 82 s. Bots are idle 30% of their alive time. This overlaps GP-03 and GP-04.
- **GP-09 Easy comeback too strong. Plausible, medium.** A team down 5-9 takedowns at the end wins 46%. This is partly by design (GAPS B3). Add an Easy deficit gate.
- **GP-07 Pixel 57.3%, Meatball 56.0%. Downgraded to low.** The confidence intervals include 54%. Confirm on the 2,000-seed gate before tuning.
- **GP-08 Bots never use some kit identities. Downgraded to low.** Gabby's E is used only to run home (`casts.ts:197-206`, `:346-349`).

## 5. Graphics, readability and performance

Performance holds 58-60 fps at p95 16.8 ms, with 47-81 draw calls, a 45 MB heap, 497 KB to the title and 746 KB to the match. Evidence: `gfx/` (screenshots plus `census.mjs`, `perf.mjs`, `tour.mjs`, `overview.mjs`).

- **GFX-01 Enemy dog structures are blue. Confirmed, downgraded to high.** `palette.ts:11` TEAM[1] is 0x3d5a9e, close to RELATION.ally 0x3d8bff. The species styling is deliberate (`palette.ts:17-20`), and the red rings released today still mark enemies, so this is a conflicting cue. Recolour the Woof cloth to mustard/brown, not everything by relation. Evidence: `gfx/d-1440x900-high-aviete-08.png`, `gfx/sheet-tour.png`, `gfx/overview-top.png`. LoL, Wild Rift and MLBB keep blue for yours and red for theirs, whatever your side.
- **GFX-04 Overhead pile-up on phones. Confirmed, high.** Plates, the Pudding chip, "Purr-ified!" and "CLANK!" stack within about 200 px. Evidence: `gfx/d-667x375-med-clem-03.png`, `gfx/d-844x390-med-brutus-01.png`. Wild Rift shows allies in combat as a bar only, and LoL shows CC as an icon on the bar.
- **GFX-05 Cat minions look like mini heroes. Confirmed, medium.** A white body in pink cloth next to Clem. Make minions a different creature class (kittens in helmets, puppies in bibs) in one flat team colour.
- **GFX-08 Med draws at 1.25x DPR on phones. Confirmed, medium.** `config.js:505`. The dynRes config has no reader. There is headroom to start Med at up to 2.0 on short-landscape screens and step down.
- **GFX-10 Low HP plus core alert plus ult sweep wash the whole screen. Confirmed, medium.** Evidence: `gfx/d-1440x900-med-sage-04.png`. Allow one full-screen layer at a time. The source layers are not identified yet.
- **GFX-03 Top-down silhouettes. Downgraded to medium.** Some heroes read as a hat or hood (`gfx/sheet-clem.png`) but are still recognisable by colour. Put crests, plumes and ear tips on top, and run a 48 px read test.
- Low or low-medium: GFX-06 plain towers, cores and camps (add stepped silhouettes and damage states). GFX-07 an empty mid meadow (only visible when zoomed out). GFX-11 the merged forest (28.5K tris, also drawn for shadows) and hedge (15K) cannot be culled; chunk them for weak devices. GFX-12 no central respawn countdown (a small RespawnCard exists). GFX-13 the lobby skin teaser overlaps the pedestal. GFX-14 fonts are 154 KB; Ext already has unicode-range, so the saving is to drop or narrow the Latin file or subset Ext, not to add a range.
