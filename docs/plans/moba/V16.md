# Pawlegends sim v16 and M1b casual PvP: implementation plan (2026-11-08)

**Status:** SIM part implemented on `feat/pawlegends-v16` (not pushed; see §9 for what shipped, what moved to v17 and the gate numbers). Backend and client M1b implemented on the same branch (§10). Not released: the sim gates in §9 still fail.
**Branch:** `feat/pawlegends-v16`, cut from `origin/main` at `a2dc4063`. Sim v15 (10 cats v 10 dogs), `PROTO 2`, Quick Play co-op (M1a) and the App Store app `com.tokentails.pawlegends` are live.
**Sources:** `PARITY.md` §3.2 to §3.13 and §8, `MAP-GAPS.md` section B, `M1A-IMPL.md`, and the code at `a2dc4063`. Paths are relative to the repo root.
**Who releases:** the main session. This lane builds and tests, and does not push or deploy.

## 0. Summary

v16 is one sim release with two parts:
- **S-PvP:** humans can hold seats on both teams, and PvP gets its own symmetric rules.
- **Navigation fixes from MAP-GAPS B:** the low-risk ones.

The map-design half of MAP-GAPS B (B7 to B10, B14) goes to the "later" list as v17 (`S-Obj`). Each of those items needs a balance re-baseline, and some need a new render pass.

M1b then adds:
- a `casual` queue to the existing M1a matchmaker, for 3v3 human PvP;
- `PROTO 3`, with 6 human slots and team-scoped votes;
- a **Co-op vs bots / PvP** mode chip on Play online;
- the §3.11 netcode measurement plan.

| Part | Ships as | Changes replays | Gate |

## 3. SIM: exact changes

### 3.1 S-PvP core (`PARITY.md` §3.12, made concrete against the v15 code)

**3.1.1 Config and validation**

`sim/api.ts` `MatchConfig` gains two optional fields:
- `mode?: 'pve' | 'pvp'`. Absent means `pve`.
- `fillDifficulty?: readonly [Difficulty, Difficulty]`. Used only in pvp: the bot profile of each team's fill seats.

`sim/party-inputs.ts` `validateHumans(humans, mode)`:
- In pve the rule is unchanged: ascending and unique, starts with 0, within {0, 1, 2}.
- In pvp:
  - ascending and unique, within 0..5;
  - contains 0 **and** 3, so each team has a lead human;
  - 2 to 6 entries.
- The relay seats team-1 humans from slot 3 upward, so slot 3 is always team 1's lead.

Other edits:
- `sim/humans.ts` `isHumanSlot`: the bound becomes `slot < 2 * SLOTS_PER_TEAM`. The mask is still a u8, so pve masks are unchanged.
- `sim/state.ts` `PartyState`: `gates` and `logs` grow to 6 entries. `createPartyState` builds one gate and one log for every human slot ≥ 1, including 3..5.

**3.1.2 Rules: a PvP row that cannot leak a bot-only or human-only buff**

`data/bots.ts` gains `PVP_RULES: DifficultyRules`, frozen. It starts from `hard` and sets every human-only or one-sided field to neutral:
- `humanDamageBp`, `humanTakenBp`, `humanRegenBp` and `humanRespawnBp`: 10000;
- `humanPanicBp` 0, `spareHuman` 0, `enemyRespawnBp` 10000, `enemyFishBp` 10000 and `enemyAheadXpBp` 10000;
- `enemyComeback` true (both teams);
- `enemyBackdoor` true (both cores guarded the same way);
- `humanCoreOpenAt` 0, `humanCoreFloorBp` 0 and `coreOpenAt` 0;
- `pushNeedsHuman` false, `towerKnockback` false, `carryAllies` 0 and `enemiesToHuman` false;
- `rallyBp` 0 and `rallyCap` 0;
- `scriptFrom` and `scriptUntil` 0, and `truceUntil` 0;
- `humanDeathCore` true;
- `forfeitFrom` 7200 (4:00, `PARITY.md` §3.7);
- `catchUpFrom` 5400, applied to both teams by `levels.ts` as today.

Code changes that go with the row:
- `sim/rules.ts`: `rulesOf(m)` returns `m.rules`. `createMatch` caches `m.rules = mode === 'pvp' ? PVP_RULES : DIFFICULTY_RULES[difficulty]`, so pve is byte-identical.
- `units/minion.ts:38` and `:98` read `DIFFICULTY_RULES[m.config.difficulty]` directly. Both now go through `rulesOf(m)`.
- **The struggling-human respawn rule** (`combat/damage.ts:233`) fires whenever `humanRespawnBp >= BP_ONE`, so the neutral row alone does **not** turn it off. Add `humanStreakRespawn: boolean` to `DifficultyRules`: true on all four pve rows, false on `PVP_RULES`, and test it in the `struggling` condition.
- **`teamOfSlot(HUMAN_SLOT)` sites that pick a side** (`damage.ts:92`, `:115`, `:226`, `fish.ts:34`, `:77`, `levels.ts:58`, `projectile.ts:79`, `minion.ts:37`, `:100`) are all neutralised by the row above.
  - A static test, `pvp-rules.test.ts`, classifies **every** `DifficultyRules` key as `neutral-in-pvp` or `symmetric` and asserts the `PVP_RULES` value.
  - Adding a rules field later without classifying it fails the test. This is the "no bot-only buff leaks" guard.

**3.1.3 A lead human per team (bots, pings, AFK leash)**

`sim/humans.ts` gains `leadOf(m, team)`:
- in pve, `HUMAN_SLOT` for both teams (as today);
- in pvp, `0` for team 0 and `3` for team 1.

The bot code replaces `HUMAN_SLOT` with `leadOf(m, w.team[id])` where a bot reasons about *its own* team's human. The sites are:
- `bots/brain.ts:148`, `:277–289` (leash and `humanLane`), `:332`, `:371`, `:565–581`, `:642`, `:750`, `:787`, `:832` and `:850`;
- `bots/orders.ts:27`, `:65`, `:83` and `:114`;
- `bots/pinger.ts`;
- `bots/index.ts:46`, which picks the profile: in pvp it uses `ALLIES[fillDifficulty[team]]` for both teams, so each team's bots play as teammates and obey pings;
- `heroes/talents.ts:184` (unchanged; humans choose their own talents).

Sites where an *enemy* bot reasons about "the human" (`brain.ts:172–195` truce, spare and peel; `:807` hunt; `:776` script) are all behind fields that are 0 or false in `PVP_RULES`. In pvp they collapse to the plain target choice.

The team-1 AFK leash (`G.HumanActAt` for team 0):
- Keep the last action tick of team 1's lead in `m.pvp.actAt1`. It is **not hashed**, like `w.humans`. It is derived from logged inputs and positions, so desync detection still covers it through positions.
- `G.HumanActAt` and `G.HumanPX/PY` keep their slot-0 meaning, which keeps pve identical.

**3.1.4 Same-tick input order (pinned by a golden, like B2)**

`sim/index.ts` `step`:
1. slot 0's ping, then slot 0's command, then slot 0's forfeit block (as today);
2. in pvp: slot 3's ping and slot 3's forfeit block;
3. `applyPartyInputs` for slots 1, 2, 4 and 5 in slot order, with slot 3's command applied at its own slot position (1, 2, 3, 4, 5);
4. `stepBots`.

`party-inputs.ts` `ownCommands` keeps `Op.Ping` and `Op.Forfeit` for slot 3 in pvp. For slots 1, 2, 4 and 5 it drops them, unlogged, as today.

**3.1.5 Per-team surrender**

- `m.pvp.forfeit1` is set by slot 3's gate from `forfeitFrom`.
- `sim/match.ts` `checkWin`, after the core checks:
  - both teams forfeit on the same tick → `tieBreakWinner`;
  - only team 0 → team 1 wins;
  - only team 1 → team 0 wins.
- Reason `'forfeit'` in every case.
- In pvp `endMatch` writes `outcome` from team 0's view, as today. `MatchResult.winner` is already a team. The backend derives each slot's result from `winner === teamOfSlot(slot)` (§4.5).

**3.1.6 Picks**

`fillPicksParty` in pvp:
- Team 0's side comes from slot 0's hero, and team 1 is the other species (the `fillPicks` rule).
- Human seats overwrite their slots; bots fill the rest from the seed as today.
- Invariant (tested): a pvp pick list never mixes species within a team. The relay checks this before `startMatch`.

**3.1.7 Replay and verification**

- `sim/api.ts` adds `PvpReplayRecord`: `v: 3`, `mode: 'pvp'`, `fillDifficulty`, `humans: PartySeat[]` (slots 0..5) and one stream per human. The other fields match `v: 2`.
- `sim/party.ts`:
  - `pvpReplayRecord(m)`;
  - `checkPvpShape`, which applies the same bounds as `checkPartyShape` plus the pvp seat rules;
  - `runPvpReplay`, the `others` array sized 6.
- `sim/server.ts`: `verifyPvpReplay`, exported on the party facade so the referee and the replay worker can call it.
- Export `PVP_SINCE = 16`.

**3.1.8 Side and spawn symmetry**

- The map is authored for team 0 and mirrored with `mirrorX` for team 1 (`data/map.ts`). Spawns, towers, camps and bushes are therefore symmetric by construction. `map-mirror.test.ts` asserts it for every list: `WALLS`, plaza, `TOWER_SPOTS`, camps, bushes, lane points and fountain.
- Which species plays on the left (team 0) is decided by the matchmaker, alternating per match (§4.1). Map-side bias therefore never becomes a faction bias.
- A known source of bias: slot order within a tick, because team 0's commands apply first. The `pvp-symmetry` test measures it. If the side win rate leaves 47–53%, the parity-alternating iteration item on the later list is pulled in. Measured after B6 (46.5%), the bias was hero step order, not command order; fixed at the root in §3.4.

### 3.4 Release-gate fixes (2026-11-08, after B6)

After the B items the PvP map side was 46.5% (gate 47–53%) and four hero checks failed the 46–54% band (Brutus 56.1%,
Duchess 45.6%, Purrify 46.7%, Brutus level-6 branch 56.9%). Two changes, both inside v16 (frozen v10–v15 untouched):

- **Side bias, root cause: hero tick order.** `stepHeroes` moved each hero in place in id order, so team 1 (slots 3–5)
  checked range against team 0's positions after team 0 had already stepped this tick and started its swing one tick
  earlier (a deterministic mirror duel went to slot 3 in 6 of 11 heroes, never to slot 0). Every hero now steps
  against the others' start-of-tick positions; each hero's own step is staged and all land together
  (`units/hero.ts`; during the step a hero writes only its own position). `tests/unit/pvp-first-strike.test.ts` pins
  it: clem, rocket and sage on slots 0 and 3 land the first hit on the same tick and die together or not at all. None
  of the B items caused it (bisect and ablations in the scratch notes); the drop at B4 was noise on this bias.
- **Hero retune for B3** (a Move kept after a dash or knockback helps chargers and divers, hurts kiting mages; B11
  hurts the bush ambusher): Brutus `takenBp` 9725 → 9875 (≈ 3 pp per +75, do not go higher), Duchess `dmgBp`
  10175 → 10550, Domino `dmgBp` 8825 → 9050 (the Brutus nerf alone dropped him to ≈ 45%), Bengal `dmgBp` 9700 → 9950,
  Purrify cooldown 2700 → 2400.

Measured with both changes (2,000 seeds per window; a = seeds 1–2000, b = 2001–4000):

| Gate | a | b |
|---|---|---|
| `pvp-symmetry` map side / cats | 49.6% / 50.6% | 48.3% / 50.4% |
| Balance gate | all pass | one fail: Sage level-6 branch 41.7% (300 slots; 48.3% on a, pooled 45.0%) |
| Brutus / Duchess / Domino / Bengal / Purrify | 50.7 / 51.1 / 52.8 / 50.4 / 48.0 | 50.0 / 50.1 / 52.0 / 50.7 / 51.4 |
| Brutus level-6 branch | 48.5% | 48.5% |
| PvE player side, median length | 49.3%, 7:14 | 51.4%, 7:13 |

The level-6 branch checks rest on ≈ 300 slots (± 2.9 pp per run), so a single 2,000-seed window can fail one of them
on noise; judge the balance gate on both windows pooled (4,000 seeds). Every match mode changed, so the vendored v16
fixtures and `party-identity.json` were regenerated.

## 4. BACKEND (M1b): matchmaker, relay, protocol, `/live`

### 4.1 Matchmaker: the `casual` queue (`backend/src/pawlegends/mm/matchmaker.ts`, pure, fake clock)

**Entry types**
- `MmEntry.queue: 'coop' | 'casual'`. `'ranked'` is still refused (M2).
- A casual entry is a solo, a duo or a trio. A trio is refused in coop as today.
- Pools stay keyed `region|queue|sim`. A casual pool exists only for sims ≥ `PVP_SINCE`.

**Eligibility**
- Newbies cannot queue casual: fewer than 5 completed matches, or `rd > 300`. They get `mm_locked{reason:'pvp_newbie'}`, and the client shows the lock before sending.
  - The relay counts **server records only**, never `hello.hint` (review fix, Nov 8): an account's replay-verified saves (`User.pawlegendsMatches`) and rated games, and any identity's completed Quick Play rooms in the last 90 days (not left, not voided). A standalone guest (guest key, no ticket) unlocks PvP after 5 completed Quick Play rooms. An IP-only identity (no ticket, no guest key) has no record and never queues casual, so dodges and leaves in PvP always land on a trackable identity.
  - The casual start rating never comes from the hint either: the casual row, else the co-op row, else the top difficulty the account has a saved match on (`seedRating`), else 1500 with `rd 300`. The e2e relay harness alone trusts `hint.matches` (`trustHintMatches`), since its browsers have no server history.
- A protected pool keeps members with `rd > 250` apart from players with `rd ≤ 250`. It applies until 10 PvP matches and widens at 45 s.

**Rating window**
- `|Δ team mean| ≤ min(75 + 12·w, 400)`, where `w` is the oldest member's wait in seconds.
- A member with `rd > 200` counts as `rd`-wide.
- Party rating: `mean + 0.25·(max − mean)`.

**Hard rules**
- **Same `ipKey` (or `netKey` in ranked) never on opposite teams.** Teammates are allowed.
- Cats and dogs never share a team.
- A side request (`cat` or `dog`) is honoured inside the window and relaxed after 25 s (`PARITY.md` §8 #5). `any` fills the short side.

**Team building** (greedy, oldest entry first, deterministic: ties broken by `id`)
1. Try 3v3 humans. Fit cost: `|predicted win% − 50|·2 + 5·side mismatch + 0.05·max RTT`. A role-overlap term is reserved and 0 until role preference exists.
2. Parties face parties of the same size or larger, until 40 s.
3. **Bot fill at `fillAt = 30 s`, fixed for casual.** Shapes in order of preference:
   - 3v3;
   - 2v2 plus one bot each side (symmetric);
   - 3v2 plus one bot;
   - 1v1 plus two bots each side;
   - 2v1 plus bots.
   - A lone human is never set against 3 humans while a 2-human team can form within 15 more seconds.
4. **No opponent human by 45 s.** The entry moves to the coop pool with its wait credited, and the card says "No PvP players right now: playing Co-op vs bots" with a **Keep waiting** button, which re-enters casual. Default for zero friction (§7).
5. Fill bot difficulty is **one level for both teams**, from the mean of every human in the match: `< 1300` easy, `< 1650` normal, else hard (`casual.ts matchFillDifficulty`, `PARITY.md` §3.2). It goes to the sim as `fillDifficulty` (both entries equal). A per-team level made 1295 v 1305 easy bots against normal bots (review fix, Nov 8).
6. **Fairness covers every shape, bots included.** The win chance is predicted on each team's three seats, each bot at its level's rating (`BOT_RATING`: easy 1200, normal 1475, hard 1800). Every casual match must be predicted inside 40–60% until the anchor waited 25 s, then inside 35–65% (`MM_CASUAL.fairBand`, `fairAfter`). The 400-point window alone no longer seats a lopsided filled 1v1 or 3v1. `mm-sim` fails on any match outside 35–65% or with different bot levels per team. Cost: about 4–7% fewer PvP matches at 6–20 joins a minute (they play co-op at 45 s instead).

**Map side**
- `team0Species` alternates per pool. It is the species that played on the left less often in the last 50 casual matches, with ties to `cat`.
- Cats are therefore team 0 only about half the time, and the A24 red-side flip (shipped, `render/coords.ts setViewTeam`) shows every player their base at the bottom left.

**Output**
- `MmMatch` gains `mode: 'casual'`, `teams: [{species, slots, members, bots, difficulty}, …]`, `team0Species` and `fill: slot[]`.
- Team-0 humans take slots 0, 1, 2 (oldest first). Team-1 humans take slots 3, 4, 5.

**Accept card (PvP only)**
- 10 s. A party skips it for its own members (D11).
- A decline or timeout is a `dodge` (`leaver.ts`): 2 free a day, then +1 min each.
- The others go back to the front of the queue (`mm.cancel{reason:'declined'}`).

**Ranked**
- Never formed in M1b. When M2 adds it, **no bot fill in ranked**. This is the instruction for this lane, and it supersedes `PARITY.md` §8 #3 (symmetric third-seat fill) until the user decides otherwise (§7).

  - No rating and no catnip for anyone.

| `frame_p95` | Render frame time | ≤ 33 ms on phones in 6-human rooms |
