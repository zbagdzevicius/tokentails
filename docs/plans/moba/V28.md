# V28: Hard bots push, leads hold, One Lane Brawl, the Duel and Boss Weekend (sim v28, 2026-11-14)

Standing goal (founder): "fully-fledged multiplayer game that's better than mobile-legends and league of legends and
wild rift". This run closes the round 2 gap rows that need no founder decision and live in the sim: the two V27 gates
that still failed (both bot behaviour), and the missing mode variety against Wild Rift, Mobile Legends and Brawl Stars
(no one-lane mode, no 1v1, no PvE boss). Sim v28 freezes v27 at 9c5c82e4; the backend verifies 10 to 28.

## 1. Bot fixes (Hearthwood only; every Crossing match plays exactly as in v27)

### 1.1 Hard groups and pushes with its waves after Fortified (`JUNGLE_BOT.groupAtHard` 9900)

Hard teams traded poke for minutes without committing (V27.md §1.D: towers first hit at ~2:50, fallen after ~4:40), so
the 5v5 Hard first tower fell at a 7:29 median (T1 target 5:00 to 7:00). A Hard team (`BotParams.hunts`) now groups in
its push lane from 5:30, half a minute after Fortified ends, instead of 8:00. Grouping at 5:00 gave 5:54, at 6:00 6:35;
5:30 keeps a margin both ways. Rookie, Easy and Normal keep 8:00. Real 5v5 PvP fill bots play Hard's row, so they group
at 5:30 too.

### 1.2 A lead is protected, not thrown (`protectsLead`: Hearthwood, BOT_AI.leadGroup (5) or more ahead)

The 13 comeback matches of the v27 down-10 windows had the leader's deaths at 2.6 times the trailer's after the biggest
gap (221 to 84), 28% of them in reach of an enemy tower, and the trailer taking more Grizzlechops and Great Puddings,
then out-levelling the leader by about 4 levels at the end. Three rules for a team protecting a lead:

1. It never chases a foe into an untanked enemy tower, not even a low one (finishHpBp no longer applies).
2. Objective focus: it leaves a siege for the Great Pudding, and every role (not only Prowl, Guard and the Fish laner)
   goes for Grizzlechop.
3. No trickling in: on the enemy half it starts no new fight with fewer than `BOT_AI.leadFightAllies` (3) of its heroes
   near; a fight already on is kept.

Tried and dropped (map5v5-probe, three 120-seed windows): rule 1 alone (11.7 / 0.0 / 11.3%), a higher fight bar when
ahead (×1.25: 3.7 / 6.7 / 9.8% with 1 and 2), no early lead grouping on Hearthwood (the first tower moved to 8:46),
also refusing dives when ahead (9.2 / 8.3%).

## 2. The Sunny Lane strip and One Lane Brawl (`onelane`)

**The strip** (`data/map.ts` CROSSING_STRIP, `PLAYFIELD.strip`): the Crossing with only Sunny Lane's band and the two
base plazas walkable; Puddle Lane, the jungle and the river between them are forest (the nav grid, the drawn trees and
the minimap all read the same `onPlayfield`). `bindMap(index, strip)` binds it; the world's mutator picks it
(`bindWorld`), the main thread binds it in `useMap(map, mutator)`. Strip mutators (`MutatorRules.strip`) spawn Sunny
Lane's waves only, no rat camps, Pudding, Kibble Cannon, yarn balls or Boing Beds; one Frenzy Catnip on the Sunny
river crossing; the Fish Snacks always on Sunny Lane. Every bot plays Sunny Lane.

**One Lane Brawl** (ARAM-like): 3v3, any champion, start at level 3, XP ×1.5, Fish ×2 ("faster gold"), no Nap and no
fountain heal (`noRecall`: Op.Nap is ignored, the fountain heals nobody, the Nap button is hidden; Fish Snacks every
15 s are the heals, bots retreat to their snack or behind their tower and come back at 65% HP), towers ×0.7, cores
×0.8, Fortified 0:45, respawns 8 s rising to 14 s, Sunset 5:00, Sudden Death 7:00, time up 8:00.

Sanity (`tools/modes-sanity.mjs`, 24 matches, Normal player-bot vs Normal): median 5:27, p90 7:06, every match a core
kill, 5.17 takedowns a minute (Skirmish 3.40), no stuck bot.

## 3. The Duel (`duel`)

1v1 on the strip with its waves (the other four heroes sit out: `playing [1, 1]`; they never spawn, are not in the
snapshot and have no portrait). The first to 2 takedowns or to the first tower wins (EndReason `goal`, 5). Start at
level 3, XP ×1.5, Fish ×1.5, outer towers ×0.6, Fortified 0:30, respawn 5 s, no recall, time up at 5:00 (towers, core
HP, takedowns). Sanity (24): median 1:58, p90 3:19, 23 goals and 1 time up, the player-bot won 50%.

## 4. Boss Weekend (`boss`)

Three heroes (the player and two bots, or a party later) against **Mega Brutus** (`data/boss.ts`): the enemy team's
first slot plays Brutus with 11,000 HP, armour 60 and ×1.25 damage, its two team-mates sit out, and its bot holds the
Sunny Lane bridge (a 13 m leash, hunting heroes within 16 m). No waves; no structure takes damage. On top of its own kit
(the brain casts it, like any bot: "bots as the boss"):

- **Ground Pound**: a 1.3 s red disc of 5.2 m round it, then 190 damage and a 0.8 s stun; every 11 s, 9 s, 6 s by phase.
- **Meteor Paw** (from phase 2): a 1.1 s red disc of 3.2 m where its prey stood, then 230; every 7 s.
- **Phases** at 66% and 33% HP: it roars (`bossPhase` event), calls 3 then 4 pups (double-HP melee minions); phase 3
  runs its cooldowns at ×0.6. **Enrage** at 5:30: every hit ×1.75. Felling it wins (`goal`); at 6:30 it wins (time up).

The telegraphs are `windup` events on slots 6 and 7 (the client draws the disc filling to the moment it lands, enemy
red, over every VFX layer). Hero bots step out of a disc once their reaction time has run (Hard sooner than Easy), never
kite into one, and fight the boss whatever the odds. The boss never regenerates and never takes a pickup. The HUD's
boss bar (name, HP, phase) takes the team score's place in the top plate (the phone footprint does not grow); the boss
model stands 2.4× tall, a little bigger each phase (`EntitySnap.boss`, host protocol fx bits 4-5).

Weekly through live ops: the tile is an event Friday 00:00 to Monday 00:00 UTC ("Next: Fri" midweek) and the featured
mode while it runs. `GET /pawlegends/config` answers `boss: {startsAt, endsAt}`; a config doc's `boss` moves the
window, `boss: null` turns it off (LIVEOPS.md). Sanity (30 each): Normal bots win 70% (median 4:54), Hard bots 67%
(5:11); every won match reached phase 3.
