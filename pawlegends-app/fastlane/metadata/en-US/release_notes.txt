A brand new look! PawLegends has a new logo and a new app icon: say hi to the crowned cat.

Also new since 1.0.0:
- Play online: jump into Quick Play with other players, or try casual PvP after your first few matches.
- A new fixed camera with a clear diagonal view of the map, for both teams.
- Hold to aim, and drag to the cancel bubble if you change your mind.
- A cleaner phone HUD, so fights are easier to read.
- Map fixes: heroes no longer get stuck in the forest, they path around the enemy fountain, and attacking from a bush now reveals you.

Cats or dogs: pick your side!
