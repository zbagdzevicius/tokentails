What's new:

- Match history: your season stats and recent matches, with K/D/A, grade and MVP. Tap a match for both teams' scoreboard and a damage graph. See your friends' history too (they choose who can see it).
- Country flags next to player names. Don't want yours shown? Turn it off in Settings.
- In-match chat: registered players 16+ can type to their team, and to everyone when the whole match is 16+. Everyone else keeps quick-chat. Mute all is one tap away.
- No more Sudden Death: a match ends when a core falls.
- Four new champions: Nova, Dusty, Barkula and Ranger.
- A new theme soundtrack, plus a Music volume slider.
- The results screen and Home now fit every phone without overlapping.
- You stay signed in after closing the app.
