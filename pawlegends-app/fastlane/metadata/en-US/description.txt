Pawlegends is a fast 3v3 arena battler: a team of cat heroes against a pack of dogs, in seven-minute matches that never run past eleven.

Pick a hero, push your lane, and knock down the dog towers before they reach yours. Every hero has a passive, three skills and an ultimate that unlocks mid-match, plus a talent to choose as they level up. Learn when to strike, when to fall back, and when to ping your team for a push.

PLAY ANYWHERE
- Plays offline against bots from the first launch. No account, no sign-up, no ads.
- Built for touch in landscape: one thumb moves, the other casts.
- Easy, Normal and Hard bots, with tips in your first matches.

PLAY WITH FRIENDS
- Make a party, share the 6-letter code, and take on the dogs together: up to three players on one team.
- Bots fill any empty seat.
- Quick-chat phrases and pings to plan the next push.

HEROES WITH CHARACTER
- Twenty heroes, ten cats and ten dogs, each with their own look, role and way to win a lane.
- Get a grade from C to S after every match and chase your best.
