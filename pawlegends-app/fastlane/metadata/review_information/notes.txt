Pawlegends is a self-contained game: all game files are inside the app and it plays offline against computer-controlled bots from the first launch. No login, no account, no purchases, no ads.

To test: tap Play, pick any hero, and play a match against the bots (about 5 to 7 minutes; the Easy difficulty is the quickest).

Party play (optional, needs the internet): tap Party to create a party; it shows a 6-letter code. A second device can join with that code. Matches with friends also work with bots filling empty seats.

The app updates its own game content (HTML, JavaScript, images) from our server, signed by us, within the app's existing purpose (guideline 2.5.2 / DPLA 3.3.1(B)). It never downloads native code.
