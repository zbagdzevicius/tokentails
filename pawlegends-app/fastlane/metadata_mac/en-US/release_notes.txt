Big update for the Mac!

- Sign in with Apple or email to keep your progress. Delete your account anytime in Settings.
- Friends: add friends by player code, see who's online, invite them to your party and chat.
- Ranked: climb from Kitten to Legend, with leaderboards.
- Match history: your season stats and recent matches. Click a match for both teams' scoreboard and a damage graph.
- Country flags next to player names (turn yours off in Settings).
- In-match chat for registered players 16+, with quick-chat for everyone and Mute all.
- No more Sudden Death: a match ends when a core falls.
- New champions, a new theme soundtrack and a Music volume slider.
- Online play: Quick Play with other players, 5v5 Classic and 3v3 PvP.

Cats or dogs: pick your side!
