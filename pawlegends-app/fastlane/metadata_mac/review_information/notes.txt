MAC APP (Mac Catalyst build of the iPhone app, same app record)
This is the Mac version of Pawlegends, built from the same project as the iPhone app. It runs in a resizable window (full screen from the green button or View > Enter Full Screen). Closing the window quits the app.

NO LOGIN NEEDED (sign-in is optional)
Everything in Pawlegends plays without an account, so there is no demo account. All game files are inside the app, and it plays offline against computer-controlled bots from the first launch.

OPTIONAL SIGN-IN (guidelines 4.8, 5.1.1(v))
Click "Sign in" in the top-right corner of the title screen (or Settings > Account). Choices: Sign in with Apple (shown first) or email and password. Signing in saves progress to the player's account and enables friends and ranked play. Any Apple ID or email can be used to test it.
Account deletion: once signed in, click your name in the top-right corner > Delete account (or Settings > Account > Delete account) and confirm. The account is deleted on our server at once. Web alternative: https://pawlegends.app/account

HOW TO PLAY (about 7 minutes, never more than 11)
1. On the title screen click "Play as <hero>" (Easy is the quickest).
2. Right-click the ground to move and right-click an enemy to attack (on a trackpad, a normal click also moves and attacks). Press Q, W and E to cast skills and R for the ultimate. Space centres the camera, Tab shows the scoreboard, Esc opens the menu.
3. Walk up a lane with your bots, knock down the dog towers, then the dogs' core to win.
"Change hero" opens the hero list. Settings is the gear icon.

PARTY PLAY (optional, needs the internet)
"Play with friends" creates a party and shows a 6-letter code; a second device (Mac or iPhone) joins with "Join with code". Friends play together on one team against bots; bots also fill empty seats. Chat rules are below.

CHAT AND USER-GENERATED CONTENT (guideline 1.2)
Guests, players under 16 and players who have not told us their age can only send fixed quick-chat phrases, stickers and map pings. Registered players aged 16+ can also type short messages (120 characters) to friends, to their party, and to their team in a match; typing to all players opens only when every human in the match is a registered 16+ account. A server filter rejects profanity, links, emails, phone numbers, @handles and addresses; sending is rate limited. Players can mute one player or "Mute all" in the chat drawer, block a player and report a message; our moderators can mute or ban the sender. Chat is kept 30 days and deleted with the account. Computer-controlled bots fill empty seats in matches.

NO PURCHASES
The app has no in-app purchases, no ads, no external purchase links, no crypto or wallet features and no donations. Nothing in the app links to a store or website to buy anything.

NO DOWNLOADED CONTENT ON THE MAC
The Mac app runs only the game files included in this build. It does not download code, game files or other resources, and it does not update itself: new versions of the Mac app come only through the Mac App Store (guideline 2.4.5).

SANDBOX AND PRIVACY
The Mac app uses the App Sandbox with outgoing network connections only (com.apple.security.network.client); it reads and writes no user files and uses no camera, microphone or location. No analytics, no tracking, no advertising identifier. Sign-in (optional) uses Firebase Authentication and keeps the email address, name and game progress for the account. Party play sends a random party key, a generated player name and match inputs to our game server only to run the match. Online, the game shows a country flag next to player names: the server keeps only the country code our CDN derives from the connection, never the IP; Settings > "Show my country flag" hides it. (details: https://pawlegends.app/privacy).
