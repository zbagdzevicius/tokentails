Paw Match is a free cat match-3 game inside the Token Tails game at [tokentails.com/game](/game): swap cat tiles, chain combos and reach the goal score before the clock runs out. It has 30 levels in six worlds and runs in a browser tab, so there is nothing to install and no app store involved.

This guide covers the worlds, every special tile and combination, fever mode, and how to climb the leaderboard on each level.

- The clock only starts on your first valid swap, so you can study the board first.

## How to start

1. Open [tokentails.com/game](/game). You can meet your cat first, or skip.
2. Press **PLAY** and pick **Paw Match** ("Match tiles, chain combos, beat the clock").
3. Pick a level. Level 1 is Kitten Starter 1.

Your first board shows a glowing pair and one line: "Drag one glowing tile onto the other. The clock waits for you." Do that and the run begins. On an uncleared level 1 you also get a one-time 15 extra seconds if the clock runs out.

## The six worlds

Each world has five levels. Later worlds give you less time, ask for a higher score and add more tile kinds to the board, which makes matches harder to find.

| World | Levels |
|---|---|
| Kitten Starter | 1 to 5 |
| Whisker Run | 6 to 10 |
| Shelter Sprint | 11 to 15 |
| Moon Paws | 16 to 20 |
| Star Nip | 21 to 25 |
| Legend Claws | 26 to 30 |

Level 1 gives you 132 seconds to reach a goal of 256. Level 30 gives you 94 seconds to reach 1,110.

## How Paw Match plays

The board is 8 by 8. Early levels use five tile kinds (hearts, paws, cat faces, catnip sprigs and fire); later levels add water and nature tiles. Swipe or drag a tile onto a neighbour to swap them. Three or more of a kind in a line clear, the tiles above fall, and any new lines they make clear too, as a cascade.

- **Goal:** reach the level's goal score before the clock hits zero.
- **Mission:** each level also asks you to collect a number of one tile kind, for example 10 catnip sprigs.
- **Queued moves:** you do not have to wait for a cascade to finish. Up to six swaps queue up and play in order ("MOVE QUEUED").
- **Time back:** clearing four or more tiles in one match adds a second to the clock.
- **Stars:** three per level, at 40%, 75% and 100% of the goal. Each star adds a second.
- **Fever:** in the last 15 seconds, if you are well on the way to the goal, fever mode starts and every match scores double ("FEVER x2"). During fever, big matches add two seconds instead of one.
- **Last chance:** if time runs out when you are very close to the goal, or no more than two tiles short of the mission, you get five more seconds, once.
- **Bonus missions:** small side tasks, such as a two-step chain or a match of four, pop up during a run and pay extra points and seconds.

## Special tiles and combinations

| You match | You get | It clears |
|---|---|---|
| 4 in a row or column | A line clearer | Its whole row (or column) |
| An L or T shape | A bomb | The 3 by 3 square around it |
| 5 or more in a line | A rainbow | Every tile of the kind you swap it with |

Specials go off when they are matched, or as soon as you swap one with a neighbour. Swap two specials together for a bigger blast:

- **Rainbow + rainbow:** clears the whole board.
- **Bomb + bomb:** two 5 by 5 blasts.
- **Bomb + line clearer:** three full rows and three full columns.
- **Rainbow + any special or tile:** every tile of that kind goes.

## Tips for high scores

1. **Study the board before your first swap.** The clock waits, so plan your first two or three moves.
2. **Look low on the board first.** A match near the bottom moves more tiles above it, which can set off cascades. The game's own tip says the same.
3. **Build specials instead of taking every small match.** A line of four is worth more than two quick threes.
4. **Combine two specials.** A bomb next to a line clearer is the strongest swap most boards offer.
5. **Hold a special for fever.** Points double in the last 15 seconds, so a rainbow set off then pays twice.
6. **Keep the mission in view.** Being within two tiles of it when time runs out gives you the last-chance seconds, and specials often clear mission tiles for you.
7. **Stuck? Wait a moment.** After a few idle seconds the game pulses a possible move, and a board with no moves reshuffles by itself.
