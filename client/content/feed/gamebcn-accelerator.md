In December 2024, Token Tails got a place in [GameBCN](https://gamebcn.co/), Barcelona's incubation program for video game studios. Our announcement at the time was titled "Beyond Entertainment".

## What GameBCN is

GameBCN helps game studios professionalise how they make games and find business opportunities. Its program combines training, mentoring and regular production follow-up, with a focus on talent, business development, production and networking.

## Why it mattered for Token Tails

Earlier programs, such as [Microsoft for Startups](/feed/news/microsoft-for-startups) and the [thirdweb accelerator](/feed/news/thirdweb-accelerator), were about technology. GameBCN is about the craft of making and shipping games: production habits, mentoring from people in the games industry, and contacts with publishers.

## Update, October 2026

Token Tails now makes several free cat games in the browser, including [Catnip Heist](/heist) and [the main game](/game). They are tied to Pink Paw, a cat shelter in Kaunas, Lithuania, and since October 2026 Token Tails pays the shelter through open-source contracts, with each payout public at [/shelter-payouts](/shelter-payouts). The full timeline is in [The Token Tails story](/feed/news/token-tails-story).
