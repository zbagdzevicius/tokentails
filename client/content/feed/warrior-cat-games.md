"Warrior cat games" usually means games inspired by the *Warriors* books by Erin Hunter: clans of wild cats, apprentices, medicine cats and leaders. The best-known options are fan-made clan simulators such as ClanGen and the official RPG *Warrior Cats: Clans of the Forest*; if you want a brave cat hero you can play right now in a browser, try Pink Paw Knight.

This page covers both: the clan games Warriors fans already love, and two free Token Tails games where small cats do brave things for other cats.

## What players mean by "warrior cat games"

Most people searching this phrase have read the *Warriors* books. The series follows four clans of forest cats (ThunderClan, WindClan, RiverClan and ShadowClan), and "Erin Hunter" is a pen name shared by a team of writers. The first book, *Into the Wild*, came out in January 2003 from HarperCollins, and the series has kept growing since ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warriors_(novel_series))).

So a "warrior cat game" can mean three things:

- **A clan simulator**: you manage a clan, its ranks, births, deaths and relationships.
- **A role-playing game**: you play one cat and grow from apprentice to warrior.
- **Any game with a brave cat hero**: a cat with a sword, a mission and someone to save.

Token Tails is not connected to the *Warriors* books, their publisher or any of the fan games below. We list them because they are what most searchers actually want.

## Fan-made Warriors games worth knowing

Fan games are made by volunteers, for free, out of love for the books. Treat them that way: download them from their own pages, and support the people who make them.

- **ClanGen.** A free, open-source clan generator and story-building game. You start a clan, then watch cats age, take apprentices, fall out and fall in love, with events written by the community. The code is public on [GitHub](https://github.com/ClanGenOfficial/clangen), which also links to the official download.
- **The itch.io warrior cats tag.** Small studios and solo makers publish browser and download games under [this tag](https://itch.io/games/tag-warrior-cats): visual novels, clan sims, character makers and short adventures. Quality varies; the fun is in the variety.
- **The official RPG.** *Warrior Cats: Clans of the Forest* is a licensed turn-based RPG with a campaign per clan and a warrior you design yourself. It was announced for 23 October 2026 on PC, PlayStation, Xbox and Switch ([RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21268-warrior-cats-clans-of-the-forest-release-date)).

If you want the full clan experience, start with ClanGen or the official RPG. If you want a short, free adventure with a hero cat and no download, read on.

## Pink Paw Knight: a tiny knight who rescues kittens

Pink Paw Knight is a third-person 3D adventure that runs in your browser. You play Clem, a tiny knight cat with a pink bow and a very large sword.

> Save lost kittens with a tiny knight. Every cat you save is a real cat at Pink Paw in Kaunas.

That line is the heart of it. The kittens Clem finds are based on real cats at Pink Paw (Rožinė pėdutė), a cat shelter in Kaunas, Lithuania. A clan story is about loyalty to your own; this one is about going out and bringing a lost cat home. For the controls and how the rescues work, read [Pink Paw Knight: a kitten rescue game](/feed/games/kitten-rescue-game).

What to expect:

- **Free, in the browser.** Open [tokentails.com/swordcat](/swordcat) and play. There is nothing to install.
- **Keyboard and mouse on a computer.** Move with WASD and look around with the mouse. The page also has touch controls for phones and tablets.
- **Real names.** The shelter cats you meet carry the names of real Pink Paw cats.

## Catnip Heist: a stealthy duo instead of a clan

If you like the strategy side of clan life, such as planning a raid and timing patrols, [Catnip Heist](/heist) scratches that itch. You guide two cats past Kibble Corp's guard dogs, grab catnip coins, open the vault and free a shelter cat.

It is a stealth puzzle game, not a fighting game. One cat holds a pressure plate while the other slips through a door, and a well-timed meow lures a guard dog away from its post. There are 16 levels in two acts, and it needs no sign-up. Our [Catnip Heist guide](/feed/games/catnip-heist-guide) has the controls and tips for three stars.
