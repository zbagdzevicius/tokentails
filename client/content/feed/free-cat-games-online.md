The quickest free cat games to play online are the five below: each one opens in a browser tab with no download and no sign-up to start. Pick Catnip Heist for stealth, Pink Paw Knight for a gentle adventure, Bad Cat Daily for 75 seconds of chaos, Moon Rave to watch a cat rave, or the main Token Tails game for a cozy pixel cat of your own.

We make all five, so this is not a neutral portal list. It is a short, honest guide to what each game is, how long it takes, and why each one is linked to a real cat shelter.

<!-- key-takeaways -->
- **Catnip Heist** ([/heist](/heist)): a two-cat stealth game, 16 levels in two acts.
- **Pink Paw Knight** ([/swordcat](/swordcat)): a tiny knight cat rescues lost kittens.
- **Bad Cat Daily** ([/badcat](/badcat)): knock things off the counter before the human turns around.
- **Moon Rave** ([/rave](/rave)): a moonlit dance floor with one cat for every $1 paid out to Pink Paw on-chain.
- **Token Tails** ([/game](/game)): meet your pixel cat and play mini-games.

## Catnip Heist: sneak two cats past the guard dogs

![Catnip Heist: a cat swaps with its partner inside a purple warehouse at night](/landing/heist-reel-poster-v1.jpg)

Guide two cats past Kibble Corp's guard dogs, grab the catnip, open the vault and free a shelter cat. It is a top-down stealth puzzle: one cat holds a door open on a pressure plate while you swap to the other, and a meow lures a guard off its post.

There are 16 heists in two acts of eight. Act 1 teaches one idea per level; Act 2 adds a new trick to each. If a guard spots you, your cat goes back to the last checkpoint, so you never lose the run, you just get a worse score.

- **Session:** about 1 to 3 minutes per level.
- **Controls:** keyboard, gamepad, or touch on phones.
- **Shelter link:** after a win, one tap and Token Tails sends Pink Paw a 0.01 USDC treat (one treat per player per UTC day). No sign-in needed.

[Play Catnip Heist](/heist), free in your browser, no sign-up needed. Stuck on a level? Read the [Catnip Heist guide](/feed/games/catnip-heist-guide).

## Pink Paw Knight: rescue lost kittens

![Pink Paw Knight: a fluffy white cat knight with a pink bow and a sword](/landing/pinkpaw-knight-trailer-poster-v1.jpg)

You play Clem, a tiny knight cat with a pink bow, in a third-person 3D adventure: one tiny knight, twelve lost kittens and one warm Rescue House. It is a calm quest about finding and bringing kittens home.

As the game says, "Every cat you save is a real cat at Pink Paw in Kaunas." Save one, and Token Tails can send Pink Paw a free treat.

[Play Pink Paw Knight](/swordcat).

## Bad Cat Daily: be the cat

![Bad Cat Daily: a grey tabby sneaks under a kitchen table while the human cooks at the stove](/landing/badcat/badcat-poster-v1.webp)

Cats are chaos. In Bad Cat Daily you are a house cat with 75 seconds to knock as much as you can off the counters, shelves and tables of today's room while the human isn't looking.

There is a new room every day, one ranked run a day with unlimited practice, and a boss room on Saturdays. You can put your own cat's face on the 3D cat from one photo, and build rooms for friends to wreck.

[Play Bad Cat Daily](/badcat). If you love the idea of your own cat on screen, see [put your cat in a game](/feed/games/put-your-cat-in-a-game).

## Moon Rave: $1 = 1 cat on the dance floor

![Moon Rave: a ginger DJ kitten in headphones under a huge green moon, with lasers over a glowing floor](/rave/og.jpg)

Moon Rave is less a game than a live scene: a ginger DJ kitten, a giant green moon and a dance floor of cats. Every $1 paid out for Pink Paw on-chain puts one more cat on the floor, so the crowd grows with real payouts.

Watching is free, and it loops forever with music made live in the browser. Anyone can add a cat for $1; how the money gets to the shelter, and who else puts cats on the floor, is in [Moon Rave: $1, one cat, one shelter](/feed/giving/moon-rave).

[Open Moon Rave](/rave).

## Why every game links to a real shelter

All five games come from Token Tails, a small Lithuanian studio that works with one real cat shelter: Pink Paw (Rožinė pėdutė) in Kaunas. Playing is free. When Token Tails sends the shelter money, from Heist treats to Moon Rave cats, the payout is recorded on-chain and listed on the public [shelter payouts page](/shelter-payouts) with a link to its transaction.

Token Tails' treat budget is 1 USDC of treats a day, so the free treats are small on purpose; the point is that each one is real and checkable. For the full picture of who pays what, read [how Token Tails works](/feed/giving/how-token-tails-works), or see more [games that help animals](/feed/giving/charity-games-that-help-animals).
