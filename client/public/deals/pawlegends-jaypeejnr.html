What counts as value you create

It counts

  • Purchases by your buyers: cosmetics store items, the Legends Pass, Tails Club and shelter cats, bought by players you bring in, for 24 months after their first purchase
  • Sponsor money you bring: cash a sponsor you introduced pays Token Tails, minus what it costs us to deliver (prizes, ads, production). It counts in the quarter it arrives
  • Counted as net: the price, minus VAT or sales tax, minus any share Token Tails sends to Pink Paw on that product, minus payment fees, minus refunds and chargebacks

How a purchase becomes yours

  • Every purchase is recorded on our server against the buyer's account. That record is the source of truth
  • It is yours when the buyer clicked your link (tokentails.com/pawlegends?utm_source=JaypeeJnr2) in the 30 days before it, or their account started from your link, or they used your creator code JaypeeJnr2 at checkout Planned
  • One purchase counts for one creator only. Your own accounts, bought or bot accounts, and refunded purchases don't count

Tracking is being built. Per-link tracking and creator codes for Paw Legends are not live yet. Until they ship, we match server purchase records to your link by hand from UTM links and checkout records. You get a statement every month with your value created, both of us sign it off, and you can ask to see the records behind any line. Each quarter's tier is set from the three signed statements.
