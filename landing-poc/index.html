Token Tails landing lab

Ten single-goal alternatives to tokentails.com. Each page has one main button, uses only verified facts, and links to flows already live on tokentails.com, tagged per variant (from=poc_vN, utm_campaign=vN).

Why the current landing feels overwhelming
  1. The page has no single job: it is a game launcher, a proof deck for judges, an impact report, a card showcase and a team page at once.
  2. The hero text is part of an image and there is no h1, so a visitor can't tell what the site is or what playing does.
  3. There is nothing to buy or donate on the landing, even though the $5 shelter cat and the $100 Legendary sale exist.
  4. The first thing a visitor reads is crypto proof meant for judges (wallets, chains, "verified" chips).
  5. About 20 calls to action lead to 14 places, and on mobile the social icons sit above the main button.
  6. Three art styles, six backgrounds, glowing headlines everywhere and about 30 autoplaying videos.
  7. The emotional hook, a real shelter cat waiting for a home, sits about 5,100px down with no way to help her.
  8. The page is about 7,000px tall and about 19 MB, and it takes about 6 s to load.

Ordered by fastest zero-spend route to revenue, scored by four independent judges on time to first dollar (35%), effort (25%), 30-day cash to Token Tails (25%) and fit with a channel we already own (15%), then checked by a critic. Plan: v3 now, v8 after Nov 27; v1 for $5 volume; v9 via Pink Paw; v2 as the free top of the funnel.
