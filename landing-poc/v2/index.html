How it works

Three beats. One good deed.

  1. Two cats, one vault

    You control two cats and swap between them. One holds the door while you swap to the other.

  2. Sneak and grab

    Slip past Kibble Corp's guard dogs, grab the catnip and free the shelter cat.

  3. A treat for Pink Paw

    Free the shelter cat and tap. Token Tails can send Pink Paw a small treat, no sign-in needed.
